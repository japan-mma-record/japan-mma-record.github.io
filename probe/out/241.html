About the event

CW 145 - Cage Warriors 145 took place on November 4, 2022 at Indigo at The O2, London, with 10 fights on the card. The main event featured Paul Hughes vs Jordan Vucenic in the Featherweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Paul Hughes defeated Jordan Vucenic by unanimous decision.
  • James Sheehan defeated Jamie Richardson by unanimous decision.
  • Modestas Bukauskas defeated Lee Chadwick by unanimous decision.

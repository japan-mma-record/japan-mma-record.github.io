About the event

CW 108 - Cage Warriors 108 took place on October 12, 2019 at Viola Arena, Cardiff, with 19 fights on the card. The main event featured Mason Jones vs Aleksi Mantykivi in the Lightweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Mason Jones defeated Aleksi Mantykivi by unanimous decision.
  • Oban Elliott defeated James Lilley by submission (rear-naked choke) in round 1.
  • Shaun Luther defeated Martin McDonough by KO (punches) in round 1.

About the event

CW 100 - Cage Warriors 100 took place on December 8, 2018 at The Viola Arena, Cardiff, with 19 fights on the card. The main event featured Jack Shore vs Mike Ekundayo in the Bantamweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Jack Shore defeated Mike Ekundayo by TKO (punches) in round 3.
  • Dean Trueman defeated Aiden Lee by TKO (punches) in round 3.
  • Aaron Khalid defeated Hakon Foss by technical submission (brabo choke) in round 2.

{{short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2021}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 262: Velasquez vs. Kielholtz |image=Bellator262.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2021|07|16}} |venue=[[Mohegan Sun Arena]] |city=[[Uncasville, Connecticut]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator 261|Bellator 261: Johnson vs. Moldavsky]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 263|Bellator 263: Pitbull vs. McKee]] }} '''Bellator 262: Velasquez vs. Kielholtz''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on July 16, 2021, at [[Mohegan Sun Arena]] in [[Uncasville, Connecticut]]. == Background == Undefeated [[Juliana Velasquez]] made her first title defense of the [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Women's Flyweight Champion|Bellator MMA Women's Flyweight World Championship]]{{Broken anchor|date=2026-11-05|bot=User:Cewbot/log/20201008/configuration|target_link=List of Bellator MMA champions#Women's Flyweight Champion|reason= }} against top-tier kickboxer [[Denise Kielholtz]]. The Brazil native has won all six of her fights in Bellator, including three by finish. Velasquez, 34, has a background in judo. Ranked #4 Kielholtz (6–2) is the [[Bellator Kickboxing#Women's Flyweight Championship|Bellator Kickboxing Women's Flyweight Championship]] and has won four straight in MMA.

This was the start of the playoffs for the [[Heavyweight (MMA)|Heavyweight]] and [[Welterweight (MMA)|Welterweight]] divisions.{{Cite web |title=2022 PFL playoff schedule includes U.K. doubleheader, promotion’s first international shows |url=https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/2022-pfl-playoff-schedule-includes-130943888.html |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=ca.sports.yahoo.com |language=en-CA}}{{Cite web |last=Lee |first=Alexander K. |date=2022-05-24 |title=PFL to make London debut for 2022 playoffs, postseason begins in New York on Aug. 5 |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/5/24/23139419/pfl-to-make-london-debut-for-2022-playoffs-postseason-begins-in-new-york-on-aug-5 |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}}{{Cite web |date=2022-05-24 |title=2022 PFL playoff schedule includes U.K. doubleheader, promotion's first international shows |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/05/2022-pfl-playoffs-international-uk-new-york |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} == Background == This will mark the second PFL playoff card, along with being the first PFL event held outside of America. The event will mark the start of the [[Heavyweight (MMA)|Heavyweight]] and [[Welterweight (MMA)|Welterweight]] playoffs, with UFC veteran and former Bellator champion [[Rory MacDonald (fighter)|Rory MacDonald]] scheduled to take on undefeated [[Magomed Umalatov]]. On the other side of the bracket, [[Sadibou Sy]] takes on Carlos Leal.{{Cite web |date=2022-05-24 |title=2022 PFL playoff schedule includes U.K. doubleheader, promotion's first international shows |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/05/2022-pfl-playoffs-international-uk-new-york |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} The Heavyweight bracket was to see Denis Goltsov taking on 2021 PFL Champion Bruno Cappelozza, while Ante Delija faced [[Renan Ferreira (fighter)|Renan Ferreira]].

{{short description|World Extreme Cagefighting mixed martial arts event in 2009}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 38: Varner vs. Cerrone |image=WEC38poster.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=January 25, 2009 |venue=[[San Diego Sports Arena]] |city=[[San Diego, California|San Diego]], California |attendance=10,201 |gate=$486,324 |buyrate= |previousevent=[[WEC 37|WEC 37: Torres vs. Tapia]] |followingevent=[[WEC 39|WEC 39: Brown vs. Garcia]] }} '''''WEC 38: Varner vs. Cerrone''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] on January 25, 2009. It aired live on the [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus Network]]. In the main event, [[List of WEC champions|WEC Lightweight Champion]] [[Jamie Varner]] defended his title against undefeated top contender [[Donald Cerrone]]. ==Background== Featured on the card was a rematch between former WEC Featherweight Champion [[Urijah Faber]] and [[Jens Pulver]]. Faber was originally lined up to face [[José Aldo]] at this event, but Aldo was replaced by Pulver. Aldo was instead moved to a bout with the debuting [[Fredson Paixao]] at this event, though Paixao was later replaced by WEC newcomer [[Rolando Perez]] due to an injury.

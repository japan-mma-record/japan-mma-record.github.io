A [[2023 in Bellator MMA#Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix]] quarterfinal bout between [[Mansour Barnaoui]] and [[Sidney Outlaw]] was scheduled to serve as the co-main event. However at the end of February, Outlaw tested positive for banned substances and was suspended, and was replaced by former [[Bellator Lightweight World Championship|Bellator Lightweight Champion]] [[Brent Primus]].{{Cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/2/21/23609221/sidney-outlaw-flunks-drug-test-brent-primus-enters-lightweight-grand-prix-at-bellator-paris|title=Sidney Outlaw flunks drug test, Brent Primus enters lightweight grand prix at Bellator Paris|website=MMAFighting.com|author=Steven Marrocco|date=2023-02-21|access-date=2023-02-21}} A bantamweight bout between [[Brett Johns]] and [[Marcirley Alves]], as well a middleweight bout between Gregory Babene and Kyle Stewart were scheduled for this event.{{Cite web |date=2023-05-01 |title=Brett Johns added to Bellator 296 card in Paris {{!}} MMA UK |url=https://mmauk.net/2023/05/01/brett-johns-added-to-bellator-296-card-in-paris/ |access-date=2023-05-02 |website=mmauk.net |language=en-GB}} However, the week of the event, Johns pulled out due to [[Staphylococcal infection|staph infection]] and Stewart was not approved by the commission due to his partial [[Near-sightedness|myopia]].{{Cite news |title=Johns withdraws from Da Silva fight |language=en-GB |work=BBC Sport |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/mixed-martial-arts/65441259 |access-date=2023-05-11}}{{Cite web |last=FightBookMMA |date=2023-05-09 |title=Bellator 296 Press Conference Quotes From The Eiffel Tower |url=https://www.fightbookmma.com/bellator-296-press-conference-quotes-from-the-eiffel-tower/ |access-date=2023-05-11 |website=FightBook MMA |language=en-US}} At weigh-ins, Steven Hill weighed in at 174.8 pounds for his fight against Nicolo Solli – 3.8 pounds over the welterweight non-title bout limit, leading to Solli to refusing to accept the bout.

{{short description|Mixed martial arts competition}} {{Infobox year in combat sports | name = Road to UFC Season 5 | image = | image_size = | alt = | caption = | promotion = [[Ultimate Fighting Championship]] | first_date = | first_date_aired = {{Start date|2026|05|28}} | last_date = | last_date_aired = | total_events = | total_fights = | title_fights = | preceded_by = [[Road to UFC Season 4]] | followed_by = }} '''Road to UFC Season 5''' is the 2026 cycle of [[Road to UFC]], a [[mixed martial arts]] (MMA) event series in which top Asian MMA prospects compete to win contracts with the [[Ultimate Fighting Championship]] (UFC).{{Cite web|url=https://www.lowkickmma.com/meng-bo-machi-fukuda-featured-in-stacked-road-to-ufc-season-5-brackets/|title=Meng Bo, Machi Fukuda featured in stacked Road to UFC Season 5 brackets|publisher=lowkickmma.com|author=Timothy Wheaton|date=2026-03-31|access-date=2026-03-31}} ==Background== The event series featured four divisions—women's strawweight, flyweight, bantamweight, and featherweight—each for which eight fighters compete in a "win-and-advance" tournament format.See [[single-elimination tournament]]. The tournament winner of each division is awarded a UFC contract. Each event within the series contains five bouts, one of which is a non-tournament match-up. The quarterfinal stage of the tournament took place over two days on May 28-29, 2026, at the [[Galaxy Macau|Galaxy Arena]] in Macau, China, with total of 10 bouts events per day. The [[Road_to_UFC_Season_4#Flyweight|Road to UFC Season 4]] flyweight final between Namsrai Batbayar and Yin Shuai took place on May 29, 2026.{{Cite web|url=https://www.lowkickmma.com/road-to-ufc-queen-shi-ming-draws-puja-tomar-for-strawweight-showcase-in-macau/|title=Road to UFC queen Shi Ming draws Puja Tomar for strawweight showcase in Macau|publisher=lowkickmma.com|author=Timothy Wheaton|date=2026-03-31|access-date=2026-03-31}} Yin Shuai, who originally lost to Aaron Tau in the semifinals, was added to the final after Tau missed weight for the February 2026 event tied to [[UFC 325]].{{Cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/story/sports/ufc/2026/01/29/ufc-325-volkanovski-vs-lopes-2-official-weigh-in-results/88424087007/|title=UFC 325 weigh-in results: Title fight set; one fight canceled|publisher=mmajunkie.usatoday.com|author=Matthew Wells|date=2026-01-30|access-date=2026-01-30}} Tau's weight miss resulted in his removal from the bout and his subsequent release, allowing Yin to step in as the replacement finalist.{{Cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/ufc/470361/ufc-cuts-ties-with-aaron-tau-after-ufc-325-weigh-in-fiasco|title=UFC cuts ties with Aaron Tau after UFC 325 weigh-in fiasco|publisher=mmafighting.com|author=Mike Heck|date=2026-02-05|access-date=2026-02-05}} Two UFC bouts took place as well: a lightweight bout between [[Rong Zhu]] and Victor Martinez, and a women's strawweight bout between [[Road to UFC Season 3#Women's Strawweight|Road to UFC Season 3]] women's strawweight winner Shi Ming and [[Puja Tomar]].

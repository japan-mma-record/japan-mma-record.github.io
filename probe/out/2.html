It was ONE's first event in the Philippines.\n\n;Background\nHeld in conjunction with the [[URCC]], ''Pride of the Nation'' has been described as the biggest event in the history of Filipino MMA as 12,523 fans attended. The event featured three former UFC champions: [[Tim Sylvia]], [[Andrei Arlovski]] and [[Jens Pulver]].{{cite web |url=https://www.mmamania.com/2012/8/2/3215833/eduard-landslide-folayang-vs-felipe-enomoto-announced-for-one-fc |title=Eduard 'Landslide' Folayang vs Felipe Enomoto announced for ONE FC: Pride of a Nation|date=2 August 2012 |publisher=mmamania.com|access-date=2012-08-02}}{{cite web |url=http://sports.inquirer.net/57261/folayang-belingon-eye-redemption |title=Folayang, Belingon eye redemption|date=7 August 2012 |publisher=Philippines Daily Inquirer|access-date=2012-08-16}}{{cite web|url=http://www.abs-cbnnews.com/sports/08/16/12/arlovski-seeks-revenge-vs-sylvia-one-fc|title=Arlovski seeks revenge vs Sylvia in ONE FC|publisher=abs-cbnnews.com/|access-date=2012-08-16|archive-date=2012-08-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120818010225/http://www.abs-cbnnews.com/sports/08/16/12/arlovski-seeks-revenge-vs-sylvia-one-fc|url-status=dead}}\n\nThere were two controversial incidents at this event which involved the use of [[soccer kick]]s. Because of this, ONE will now allow their fighters to use them at any point in a fight from ONE FC: Rise of Kings. One incident was during the bout between [[Andrei Arlovski]] and [[Tim Sylvia]]. Arlovski knocked Sylvia down and then threw soccer kicks at his head. Everyone thought the referee had stopped the fight; he actually called time because he didn't call \"open attack\". When it was decided after a five-minute rest period for Sylvia that he could not continue, the fight resulted in a [[No contest (combat sports)|No Contest]].{{cite web |url=https://www.espn.com/mma/story/_/id/8333082/confusion-reigned-supreme-one-fc |title=Confusion reigned at ONE FC|work=espn.com|date=3 September 2012 |access-date=2012-09-03}} The other incident occurred during the fight between Shannon Wiratchai and Mitch Chilson, in which Wiratchai won. However, after a thorough review of the match days after the event, ONE officials decided that Wiratchai landed an illegal leg kick to a grounded Chilson in the second round and referee Moritaka Oshiro incorrectly called an end to the fight as he determined at that point that Chilson could not properly defend himself. The win for Wiratchai was reversed and the fight officially ruled as a No Contest.{{cite web|url=http://www.sportsnet.ca/mma/2012/09/04/one_fc_wiratchai_shannon_chilson_mitch/|title=ONE FC RULES

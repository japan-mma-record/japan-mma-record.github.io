|access-date=2023-01-23 |website=Sherdog |language=en}} == Background == The event marked the promotion's 13th visit to Temecula and first since [[2019 in Bellator MMA#Bellator 229|Bellator 229]] in October 2019.{{Cite web |last=Heck |first=Mike |date=2023-01-23 |title=Marcelo Golm vs. Daniel James, Cat Zingano vs. Leah McCourt set for Bellator 293 |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/1/23/23567871/marcelo-golm-vs-daniel-james-cat-zingano-vs-leah-mccourt-set-for-bellator-293 |access-date=2023-07-31 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en-US}} The main event of Bellator 293 was a heavyweight match between No. 5-ranked [[Marcelo Golm]] and No. 8-ranked [[Daniel James (fighter)|Daniel James]]. Top-ranked [[Cat Zingano]] will face the No. 4-ranked [[Leah McCourt]] in the co-main event of the card. The main card will also include a middleweight battle between No. 4-ranked [[John Salter]] and No. 6-ranked Aaron Jeffrey and a welterweight matchup between No. 10-ranked Jaleel Willis and [[Rustam Khabilov]], a 13-fight UFC veteran who will be making his Bellator debut. A lightweight bout between Mandel Nallo and Jay Jay Wilson was scheduled for this event; however Wilson pulled out of the bout and was replaced by [[Adam Piccolotti]].{{Cite web |title=With Jay Jay Wilson out, Adam Piccolotti (13-5) has stepped in to fight Mandel Nallo (9-2) |url=https://twitter.com/mma_kings/status/1628237435338465280?s=20 |access-date=2023-02-22 |website=Twitter |language=en}} A welterweight bout between [[Rustam Khabilov]] and Jaleel Willis was scheduled for this event. However on the day of weigh-ins, Khabilov pulled out of the bout due to undisclosed reasons.{{Cite web |last=Sherdog.com |title=Bellator 293 Weigh-in Results: All Fighters Make Weight; 1 Fight

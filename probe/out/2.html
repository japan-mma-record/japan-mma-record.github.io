{{short description|WEC MMA event in 2008}} {{Use mdy dates|date=April 2025}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 35: Condit vs. Miura |image=WEC35-3titles.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=August 3, 2008 |venue=[[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] |city=[[Paradise, Nevada]] |attendance=1,006 |gate=$116,900 |buyrate= |previousevent=[[WEC 34|WEC 34: Faber vs. Pulver]] |followingevent=[[WEC 36|WEC 36: Faber vs. Brown]] }} '''''WEC 35: Condit vs. Miura''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] that took place on August 3, 2008 at the [[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] in [[Paradise, Nevada|Paradise]], [[Nevada]]. The event drew an estimated 423,000 viewers on [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus]].{{cite web | url=http://www.tapology.com/fightcenter/events/wec-35-condit-vs-miura | title=WEC 35: Condit vs Miura | MMA Event }} The event featured three title fights, the first time a [[Zuffa, LLC]] promoted MMA card featured more than two title fights on the same card since [[UFC 33]] in 2001; there would not be another UFC event with more than two until [[UFC 205]] in 2016, after Zuffa had sold the promotion. ==Background== A featherweight bout between [[Cub Swanson]] and [[Hiroyuki Takaya]] was originally scheduled for this event, but Swanson pulled out due to a broken hand. Promotional newcomer [[LC Davis]] had verbally agreed to replace Swanson, but Takaya did not approve the new matchup, and the bout was removed from the card.{{cite web

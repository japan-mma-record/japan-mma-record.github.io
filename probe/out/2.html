The co-main event featured a lightweight bout between former [[UFC Lightweight Champion|UFC lightweight champion]] [[Anthony Pettis]] and fellow UFC veteran [[Stevie Ray (fighter)|Stevie Ray]]. |Featherweight |[[Brendan Loughnane]] |def. |Ago Huskić |Decision (unanimous) (29–28, 29–28, 30–27) |3 |5:00 | }} {{MMAevent end|notes=yes}} == Standings After Event == The PFL points system is based on results of the match. The winner of a fight receives 3 points. If the fight ends in a draw, both fighters will receive 1 point. The bonus for winning a fight in the first, second, or third round is 3 points, 2 points, and 1 point respectively. The bonus for winning in the third round requires a fight be stopped before 4:59 of the third round. No bonus point will be awarded if a fighter wins via decision. For example, if a fighter wins a fight in the first round, then the fighter will receive 6 total points. A decision win will result in three total points.

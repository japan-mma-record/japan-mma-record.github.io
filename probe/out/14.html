It was held at the [[Nationwide Arena]] in [[Columbus, Ohio|Columbus]], Ohio{{cite web|url=http://www.wec.tv/index.cfm?fa=eventdetail.fightCard&eid=2685|title=WEC: Bowles vs. Cruz |publisher=wec.tv|access-date=December 15, 2009}} ==Background== The only WEC event held in Ohio, WEC 47 coincided with the [[Arnold Classic|Arnold Sports Festival]], which annually draws up to 150,000 sports fans to [[Columbus, Ohio|Columbus]], Ohio.{{cite web|url=http://www.watchkalibrun.com/2009/12/14/1200946/its-official-wec-47-set-for|title=It's Official! WEC 47 set for Columbus, Ohio on March 6th |publisher=watchkalibrun.com|date=December 14, 2009}} Previously, the [[Ultimate Fighting Championship|UFC]] held three cards – ''[[UFC 68]]'', ''[[UFC 82]]'' and ''[[UFC 96]]'' – in Columbus during this festival. [[Diego Nunes (MMA)|Diego Nunes]] was originally slated to face [[Leonard Garcia]] at this event, but was forced from the card with an injury.{{cite web|url=http://www.mmamania.com/2010/2/18/1315534/report-injury-forces-diego-nunes|title=Injury forces Diego Nunes to withdraw from WEC 47 on March 6 in Columbus |publisher=mmamania.com|date=February 18, 2010}} Nunes was later replaced by [[George Roop]].{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/18019/george-roop-tapped-as-late-replacement-faces-leonard-garcia-at-wec-47.mma|title=George Roop tapped as late replacement, faces Leonard Garcia at WEC 47|publisher=mmajunkie.com|date=February 20, 2010|access-date=February 22, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100223064725/http://mmajunkie.com/news/18019/george-roop-tapped-as-late-replacement-faces-leonard-garcia-at-wec-47.mma|archive-date=February 23, 2010|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} [[Bryan Caraway]] was expected to face [[Fredson Paixao]] at this event, but was replaced by [[Courtney Buck]] after injuring his knee.{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17914/courtney-buck-replaces-injured-brian-caraway-wec-47-fight-card-now-complete.mma|title=Courtney Buck replaces injured Brian Caraway, WEC 47 fight card now complete|publisher=mmajunkie.com|date=February 11, 2010}} The Paixao/Caraway bout was rescheduled for [[WEC 50]] that August, where Paixao won via split decision. [[Stephan Bonnar]] filled in for [[Frank Mir]] as color commentator, due to training commitments for Mir.{{Cite web|url=http://www.mmamania.com/2010/3/4/1356376/frank-mir-out-stephan-bonnar-in|title=Frank Mir out, Stephan Bonnar in for WEC 47 color commentary|date=4 March 2010}} Bonnar was named as the promotion's final permanent color commentator shortly afterwards. This was the last WEC event to feature announcer [[Joe A. Martinez|Joe Martinez]] as the regular ring announcer, due to family reasons and to focus full-time on his work with [[Golden Boy Promotions]].{{cite web|url=http://www.mmafighting.com/2010/05/18/announcer-joe-martinez-leaves-wec/|title=Announcer Joe Martinez Leaves WEC|publisher=mmafighting.com|date=May 18, 2010}} Martinez later made a one-off return at [[WEC 52]], as his replacement [[Bruce Buffer]] was in [[Germany]] to announce [[UFC 122]].

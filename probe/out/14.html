Who won Cage Warriors 154: Rome?

Caolan Loughran defeated Dylan Hazan by TKO in round 2 (1:49) in the Bantamweight title main event of Cage Warriors 154: Rome, held Saturday, May 6, 2023 at Fiera Roma, Rome.

Cage Warriors 154: Rome was an MMA event from Cage Warriors Fighting Championship, held Saturday, May 6, 2023 at Fiera Roma, Rome, Italy. A championship title was on the line. 154 is part of Cage Warriors's numbered event series. 15 fights took place on the card.

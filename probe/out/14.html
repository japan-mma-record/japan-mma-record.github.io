{{Short description|Mixed martial arts event}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=PFL 7 |image=PFL_7-_2023_Playoffs.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Professional Fighters League]] |date=August 4, 2023 |venue=[[Port San Antonio#Boeing Center at Tech Port|Boeing Center at Tech Port]] |city=[[San Antonio, Texas]], United States |attendance= |estimatedviewers= |previousevent=[[PFL Europe 2 (2023)|PFL Europe 2]] |followingevent=[[PFL 8 (2023 season)|PFL 8]] }} {{Main|2023 in Professional Fighters League}} '''''PFL 7''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by the [[Professional Fighters League]] that took place on August 4, 2023, at the [[Port San Antonio#Boeing Center at Tech Port|Boeing Center at Tech Port]] in [[San Antonio, Texas]], United States. This marked the semi-final playoffs for the [[Featherweight]] and [[Light heavyweight|Light Heavyweight]] divisions.{{Cite web |last=Newswire |first=MMA Fighting |date=2023-06-29 |title=PFL announces full playoff card for Aug. 4 with featherweights and light heavyweights competing |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/6/29/23778471/pfl-announces-full-playoff-card-for-aug-4-with-featherweights-and-light-heavyweights-competing |access-date=2023-06-30 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}}{{Cite web |date=2023-06-30 |title=PFL announces featherweight, light heavyweight playoff matchups for San Antonio |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2023/06/pfl-playoffs-2023-featherweight-light-heavyweight-matchups-san-antonio |access-date=2023-06-30 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}}{{Cite web |last=Sherdog.com |title=PFL Sets 10-Bout Lineup for First 2023 Playoffs Event on Aug. 4 |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/PFL-Sets-10Bout-Lineup-for-First-2023-Playoffs-Event-on-Aug-4-190384 |access-date=2023-06-30 |website=Sherdog |language=en}} == Background == The event was headlined by [[Bubba Jenkins]] facing off against [[Jesus Pinedo]] in an attempt to reach the featherweight finals. Pinedo secured his playoff spot after stunningly knocking out [[Brendan Loughnane]], the 2022 PFL champion, in the first round. Jenkins, who lost his bid for the PFL title to Loughnane in 2022, aimed to make it to the finals again this August. The featherweight bracket's other pairing was to involve former PFL champion [[Movlid Khaybulaev]] and Gabriel Braga, battling for a spot in this year's finals. However Khaybulaev pulled out due to undisclosed reasons and was replaced by [[Chris Wade (fighter)|Chris Wade]].{{Cite web |last=Al-Shatti |first=Shaun |date=2023-07-19 |title=Chris Wade replaces Movlid Khaybulaev in PFL welterweight playoffs|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/7/19/23800651/chris-wade-replaces-movlid-khaybulaev-in-pfl-welterweight-playoffs |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}} In the light heavyweight division, Josh Silveira competed against Ty Flores, and Marthin Hamlet squared off with ex-UFC fighter [[Impa Kasanganay]]. At weigh-ins, [[Jesus Pinedo]] weighed in at 146.4 pounds, 0.4 pounds over the Featherweight limit. He was fined 20 percent of his purse, which went to his opponent Jenkins, and he started the bout with a one point subtraction.

{{short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2021}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 264: Mousasi vs. Salter |image=Bellator264.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2021|08|13}} |venue=[[Mohegan Sun Arena]] |city=[[Uncasville, Connecticut]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator 263|Bellator 263: Pitbull vs. McKee]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 265|Bellator 265: Kongo vs. Kharitonov]] }} '''Bellator 264: Mousasi vs. Salter''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on August 13, 2021 at the [[Mohegan Sun Arena]] in [[Uncasville, Connecticut]].{{Cite web|last=Lee|first=Alexander K.|date=2021-06-25|title=Gegard Mousasi vs. John Salter title fight to headline Bellator 264|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/6/25/22550746/gegard-mousasi-vs-john-salter-title-fight-to-headline-bellator-264|access-date=2021-06-26|website=MMA Fighting|language=en}} == Background == Current [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Middleweight World Championship|Bellator Middleweight World Champion]] [[Gegard Mousasi]] made his first title defense against #1 ranked [[John Salter]]. Most recently, Mousasi defeated former [[List of Bellator champions#Welterweight World Championship|Bellator Welterweight World Champion]] Douglas Lima, clearing his path to a shot at a second middleweight title run. Salter entered his title shot riding a three-fight winning streak with victories over Chidi Njokuani, Andrew Kapel and Mousasi’s teammate Costello van Steenis.

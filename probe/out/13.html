{{short description|WEC MMA event in 2010}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 46: Varner vs. Henderson |image= WEC 46 Varner vs. Henderson 2 poster.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=January 10, 2010 |venue= [[ARCO Arena]] |city= [[Sacramento, California]] |attendance=10,027{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17510/wec-46-draws-10027-attendees-for-550000-gate.mma|title="WEC 46: Varner vs. Henderson" draws reported 10,027 attendees for $550K gate|publisher=MMAjunkie.com|access-date=February 11, 2010|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130129005606/mmajunkie.com/news/17510/wec-46-draws-10027-attendees-for-550000-gate.mma|archive-date=January 29, 2013|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} |gate=$550,000 |buyrate= |previousevent=[[WEC 45|WEC 45: Cerrone vs. Ratcliff]] |followingevent=[[WEC 47|WEC 47: Bowles vs. Cruz]] }} '''''WEC 46: Varner vs. Henderson''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] on January 10, 2010. It took place at the [[ARCO Arena]] in [[Sacramento, California]].{{cite web|url=http://www.wec.tv/index.cfm?fa=eventdetail.fightCard&eid=2536|title=WEC: Varner vs. Henderson|publisher=www.wec.tv/index.cfm |access-date=November 2, 2009}} ==Background== [[Mark Hominick]] was scheduled to face [[Yves Jabouin]] on this card, but Jabouin has been forced off the card with an injury.{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17232/yves-jabouin-injured-mark-hominick-awaits-new-wec-46-opponent.mma|title=Yves Jabouin injured, Mark Hominick awaits new WEC 46 opponent|publisher=mmajunkie.com|access-date=December 16, 2009|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120724094834/mmajunkie.com/news/17232/yves-jabouin-injured-mark-hominick-awaits-new-wec-46-opponent.mma|archive-date=July 24, 2012|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} Jabouin was replaced by WEC newcomer [[Bryan Caraway]].{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17325/mark-hominick-vs-wec-newcomer-bryan-caraway-targeted-for-wec-46.mma|title=Mark Hominick vs. WEC newcomer Bryan Caraway targeted for WEC 46|publisher=mmajunkie.com|access-date=December 24, 2009|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120715195758/mmajunkie.com/news/17325/mark-hominick-vs-wec-newcomer-bryan-caraway-targeted-for-wec-46.mma|archive-date=July 15, 2012|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} Hominick and Jabouin would later face off at [[WEC 49]] on June 20, 2010, with Hominick winning via TKO. [[Wagnney Fabiano]] was scheduled to fight [[Frank Gomez]] at this event, however Gomez withdrew with an undisclosed injury{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17359/frank-gomez-forced-out-of-wec-46-wagnney-fabiano-waits-on-new-opponent.mma|title=Frank Gomez forced out of WEC 46; Wagnney Fabiano waits on new opponent|publisher=mmajunkie.com|access-date=December 30, 2009|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120710123213/mmajunkie.com/news/17359/frank-gomez-forced-out-of-wec-46-wagnney-fabiano-waits-on-new-opponent.mma|archive-date=July 10, 2012|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} and was replaced by Clint Godfrey.{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17369/clint-godfrey-signs-with-wec-meets-wagnney-fabiano-at-wec-46.mma|title=Clint Godfrey signs with WEC, meets Wagnney Fabiano at WEC 46|publisher=mmajunkie.com|access-date=December 30, 2009|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120724000939/mmajunkie.com/news/17369/clint-godfrey-signs-with-wec-meets-wagnney-fabiano-at-wec-46.mma|archive-date=July 24, 2012|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} The Fabiano/Gomez bout was also rescheduled for WEC 49, where Fabiano won by unanimous decision. Bantamweight contests pitting [[Eddie Wineland]] against [[Rafael Rebello]] and WEC newcomer [[George Roop]] against the returning Jesse Moreng were both scratched from this card after Rebello and Moreng both withdrew from the event. Rather than find new opponents, Wineland and Roop were instead matched up against each other on this card.{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17323/rafael-rebello-out-george-roop-vs-eddie-wineland-in-the-works-wec-46-prelims.mma|title=Rafael Rebello out; George Roop vs. Eddie Wineland in the works WEC 46 prelims|publisher=mmajunkie.com|access-date=December 24, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091226112951/http://mmajunkie.com/news/17323/rafael-rebello-out-george-roop-vs-eddie-wineland-in-the-works-wec-46-prelims.mma|archive-date=December 26, 2009|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} A lightweight bout between [[Dave Jansen]] and [[Bendy Casimir]] was once linked to this event, though it was later confirmed that Jansen would instead be facing [[Kamal Shalorus]].{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17029/undefeated-dave-jansen-returns-at-wec-46-to-fight-kamal-shalorus-not-bendy-casimir.mma|title= Dave Jansen returns at WEC 46 to fight Kamal Shalorus (not Bendy Casimir)|publisher=mmajunkie.com |access-date=November 30, 2009}} This was the last WEC card to feature former [[List of UFC champions|UFC Heavyweight Champion]] [[Frank Mir]] on color commentary.

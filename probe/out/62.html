Day one of the Craig Jones Invitational 2 is in the books. Eight teams comprising of some of the most elite grapplers in the world entered the pit in their bid for the $1,000,000 prize, and after a splendid night of action, the final four will battle once more as the tournament heads towards its thrilling conclusion. In the semi finals, New Wave takes on Atos, while on the opposite end of the bracket, B-Team locks legs with Team Australasia.

Along with the most high-stakes team event in all of competitive Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, two surging female prospects in Helena Crevar and Sarah Galvao will square off for a massive reward of $100,000 in the finals of the one versus one women’s tournament.

In the main event of the evening, promoter and superstar of the sport, Craig Jones will grapple with UFC legend Chael Sonnen in a highly-anticipated grudge match.

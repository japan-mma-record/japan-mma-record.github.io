CW 94 - Cage Warriors 94 took place on June 16, 2018 at Lotto Arena, Antwerp, with 15 fights on the card. The main event featured Ilia Topuria vs Brian Bouland in the Bantamweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Ilia Topuria defeated Brian Bouland by Technical Submission (Anaconda Choke) in round 1.
  • Jai Herbert defeated Erdi Karatas by Decision (Unanimous).
  • Krzysztof Klaczek defeated Sameer Alekozai by Decision (Unanimous).

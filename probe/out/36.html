A lightweight bout between [[Adam Piccolotti]] and promotional newcomer [[Mansour Barnaoui]] headlined the event.{{Cite web |date=2022-08-10 |title=Adam Piccolotti vs. Mansour Barnaoui headlines Bellator 287 in Milan, Italy |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/08/bellator-287-milan-italy-adam-piccolotti-vs-mansour-barnaoui-main-event-mma-announcement-matchup-booking |access-date=2022-08-10 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} A middleweight bout between Steven Hill and Walter Pugliesi was booked for this event, but Pugliesi was forced to pull out due to personal issues and was replaced by Andrea Fusi in a catchweight bout at 82 kg.{{Cite web |date=2022-10-12 |title=Walter Pugliesi salta Bellator 287: al suo posto Andrea Fusi |url=https://www.theshieldofsports.news/walter-pugliesi-salta-bellator-287/ |access-date=2022-10-12 |language=it-IT}} A heavyweight bout between Denis Stojnić and Oleg Popov was scheduled for this event, however the bout was called citing "Covid-19 protocols".

Who won UNDERGROUND #3?

Tereza Bleda defeated Lucie Pudilova by Unanimous Decision in round 3 (3:00) in the Women's Bantamweight main event of UNDERGROUND #3, held Saturday, May 30, 2020 at EPIC, Prague.

UNDERGROUND #3 was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, May 30, 2020 at EPIC, Prague, Czechia. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. 10 fights took place on the card.

Who won OKTAGON 26: NARUSZCZKA vs Krištofič?

OKTAGON 26: NARUSZCZKA vs Krištofič was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, July 24, 2021 at Štvanice, Prague, Czechia. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. 26 is part of OKTAGON's numbered event series. 12 fights took place on the card.

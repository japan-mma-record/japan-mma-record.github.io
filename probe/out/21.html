[[Kyoji Horiguchi]], meanwhile, competed under the [[Bellator MMA|Bellator]] banner for the first time as a rostered fighter on a multifight deal. In June 2019, Horiguchi was lent by [[Rizin Fighting Federation|RIZIN]] to Bellator for a one-off fight against [[Darrion Caldwell]] – a bout Horiguchi won by unanimous decision to become champion. He later vacated the title after Horiguchi suffered a knee injury that forced him to relinquish the title while he was recovering from surgery.

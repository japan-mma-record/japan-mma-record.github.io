CWFC 88 - Cage Warriors Fighting Championship 88 took place on October 28, 2017 at Echo Arena, Liverpool, with 14 fights on the card. The main event featured Chris Fishgold vs Alexander Jacobsen in the Lightweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Chris Fishgold defeated Alexander Jacobsen by Submission (Rear-Naked Choke) in round 1.
  • Lee Chadwick defeated Victor Cheng by Decision (Unanimous).
  • Soren Bak defeated Martin Stapleton by Submission (Rear-Naked Choke) in round 1.

CW 98 - Cage Warriors 98 took place on October 20, 2018 at Genting Arena, Birmingham, with 14 fights on the card. The main event featured Ross Houston vs Stefano Paterno in the Welterweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Ross Houston defeated Stefano Paterno by Decision (Split).

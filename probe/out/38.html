CW 90 - Cage Warriors 90 took place on February 24, 2018 at Echo Arena, Liverpool, with 17 fights on the card. The main event featured Molly McCann vs Bryony Tyrell in the Flyweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Molly McCann defeated Bryony Tyrell by TKO (Punches) in round 2.
  • Alexander Jacobsen defeated Lawrence Fitzpatrick by TKO (Punches) in round 1.

Background == The event marked the second co-promotion between Bellator MMA and the Rizin Fighting Federation, and Bellator's third visit to [[Saitama (city)|Saitama]]; following [[Bellator MMA vs. Rizin]] in December 2022, and [[2019 in Bellator MMA#Bellator 237|Bellator 237]] on December 29, 2019.{{Cite web |last=Raimondi |first=Marc |date=2023-05-27 |title=AJ McKee vs. Patricky Freire |language=en}} A [[2023 in Bellator MMA#Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix]] quarterfinal bout between former [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Featherweight World Championship|Bellator Featherweight Champion]] [[A. J. McKee]] and former [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Lightweight World Championship|Bellator Lightweight Champion]] [[Patricky Pitbull]] was scheduled to headline the event. However, on July 26, it was announced that McKee had pulled out of the bout due to medical issues and was replaced by the incumbent [[Rizin Fighting Federation#Lightweight Championship|Rizin Lightweight Champion]] [[Roberto de Souza]] at a catchweight of 161 pounds.{{cite web |last1=Wells |first1=Matthew |title=Major changes to Bellator X Rizin 2: A.J.

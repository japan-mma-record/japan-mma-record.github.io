The event marked Bellator's first collaboration with the Japanese [[Rizin Fighting Federation]]; the preliminary matches featured Rizin fighters. The event was headlined by a heavyweight bout between former Pride FC Heavyweight Champion [[Fedor Emelianenko]] and former [[UFC Light Heavyweight Championship|UFC Light Heavyweight Champion]] [[Rampage Jackson|Quinton Jackson]].{{cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2019/10/fedor-emelianenko-vs-rampage-jackson-to-headline-bellator-japan-on-dec-29|work=mmajunkie.com|title=Fedor Emelianenko vs. Rampage Jackson to headline Bellator Japan on Dec. 29|date=October 9, 2019}} A 160 pound catchweight bout between former [[List of Bellator MMA champions|Bellator Lightweight Champion]] [[Michael Chandler]] and former [[World Extreme Cagefighting|WEC]] and [[UFC Lightweight Champion]] [[Benson Henderson]] was expected to take place at the event.{{cite web |last1=Marrocco |first1=Steven |title=Michael Chandler vs. Benson Henderson 2 co-headlines Bellator Japan |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2019/10/25/20933138/michael-chandler-vs-benson-henderson-2-co-headlines-bellator-japan |website=[[MMAFighting.com]] |date=25 October 2019 |access-date=5 December 2019}} It was to have been a rematch from [[Bellator MMA in 2016|Bellator 165]] where Chandler won by split decision over Henderson to remain the lightweight champion.{{cite web |last1=Dyce |first1=Mike |title=Bellator 165: Michael Chandler defeats Benson Henderson |url=https://www.si.com/mma/2016/11/20/bellator-165-michael-chandler-defeats-benson-henderson |website=[[SportsIllustrated.com]] |access-date=5 December 2019}} However, Henderson pulled out on December 5 due to injury.

This was the final WEC event, due to the merger with the UFC.\n\n==Background==\nThis was the final WEC event before merging with the [[Ultimate Fighting Championship]] in 2011.{{Cite web|url=http://www.bloodyelbow.com/2010/10/28/1779797/dana-white-to-announce-ufc-merger-with-the-wec#comments|title=Dana White To Announce UFC Merger With the WEC|date=28 October 2010|publisher=Bloody Elbow|access-date=28 October 2010|archive-date=31 October 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101031081215/http://www.bloodyelbow.com/2010/10/28/1779797/dana-white-to-announce-ufc-merger-with-the-wec#comments|url-status=dead}}\n\nThis event was unique in that the WEC and [[AMP Energy]] allowed fans to choose the location of the event.{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/19393/wec-and-amp-energy-allowing-fans-to-determine-location-of-december-fight-card.mma|title=WEC and AMP Energy allow fans to determine location of December's WEC 53 event}} The fans were given 30 options and the finalists were [[Milwaukee]], [[Nashville]] and [[Phoenix, Arizona|Phoenix]]. During the live telecast of [[WEC 50]], Phoenix was announced as the winner of the contest.{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/20344/hometown-takedown-sends-wec-to-phoenix-area-henderson-likely-for-card.mma|title=\"Hometown Takedown\" sends WEC to Phoenix; Henderson likely for WEC 53 card|publisher=MMAjunkie.com|date=August 19, 2010|access-date=August 20, 2010|archive-date=August 20, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100820122059/http://mmajunkie.com/news/20344/hometown-takedown-sends-wec-to-phoenix-area-henderson-likely-for-card.mma|url-status=dead}}\n\n[[Maciej Jewtuszko]] was expected to face [[Ricardo Lamas]] at this event.{{cite web|url=http://www.mmanews.pl/2010/mmanews-pl-znamy-imie-kolejnego-przeciwnika-macieja-jewtuszko-na-grudniowa-gale-wec/comment-page-1/|title=MMAnews.pl knows the next opponent of Maciej Jewtuszko (Polish)|publisher=mmanews.pl|date=October 20, 2010}} However, Jewtuszko had to withdraw as he suffered a broken hand during training.{{cite web|url=http://www.mmarocks.pl/2010/10/28/maciej-jewtuszko-zlamal-reke/|title=Maciej Jewtuszko has broken his hand (Polish)|publisher=mmarocks.pl|date=October 28, 2010}} Jewtuszko was replaced by WEC newcomer [[Iuri Alcântara]].{{cite web| url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/21268/ricardo-lamas-meets-newcomer-yuri-alcantara-at-wec-53-in-december.mma| title=Ricardo Lamas meets newcomer Yuri Alcantara at WEC 53 in December| publisher=mmajunkie.com|date=November 4, 2010}}\n\nThis event was named [[Sherdog]]'s 2010 Event of the Year.{{cite web |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/articles/1/Sherdogs-2010-Misc-Awards-29168 |title=Sherdog's 2010 Misc. Awards Event of the Year |last1=Sherwood |first1=Jeff |work=[[Sherdog]] |access-date=April 25, 2011}}\n\nThe event drew an average of 615,000 viewers on [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus]].{{Cite web|url=https://www.tapology.com/fightcenter/events/wec-53-henderson-vs-pettis|title=WEC 53: Henderson vs. Pettis | MMA Event}}\n\n==UFC Lightweight title eliminator==\nThe winner of the WEC Lightweight Championship fight between reigning champion [[Benson Henderson]] and challenger [[Anthony Pettis]] was scheduled to face the winner of the [[UFC 125]] [[Frankie Edgar]] [[List of UFC champions#Lightweight Championship|(C)]] vs. [[Gray Maynard]] title fight in order to unify the WEC and UFC lightweight championships. However, the Edgar/Maynard fight ended in a draw necessitating a [[UFC 136]] rematch (which Edgar won). Though Anthony Pettis won the final WEC lightweight title bout, defeating Henderson via unanimous decision, he opted not to wait for the winner of Edgar/Maynard rematch and instead fought [[Clay Guida]] at [[The Ultimate Fighter 13 Finale]], losing by unanimous decision and negating his promised title shot (though he would win the UFC Lightweight Championship in his own right over Henderson in August 2013.)\n\n==UFC Bantamweight Championship==\nThe winner of the Bantamweight Championship fight between reigning champion [[Dominick Cruz]] and challenger [[Scott Jorgensen]] determined ''both'' the final WEC Bantamweight Champion and the inaugural [[List of UFC champions#Bantamweight Championship|UFC Bantamweight Champion]].{{cite web|url=https://arizona.sbnation.com/2010/12/14/1876904/wec-53-dominick-cruz-scott-jorgensen-fight-to-be-first-ufc|title=WEC 53: Dominick Cruz, Scott Jorgensen Fight To Be First UFC Bantamweight Champion|publisher=sbnation.com|date=December 14, 2010}} This is the only UFC title fight to ever take place on another promotion's card, and the first UFC title bout to air on free television since [[UFC 75]] in July 2007.

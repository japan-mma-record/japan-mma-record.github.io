end|notes=yes}}\n{{cite web |title=PFL 1 |url=https://pflmma.com/event/2024-pfl-1|website=www.pflmma.com |access-date=11 March 2024}}\n\n== Standings after event==\nThe PFL points system is based on results of the match. The winner of a fight receives 3 points. If the fight ends in a draw, both fighters will receive 1 point. The bonus for winning a fight in the first, second, or third round is 3 points, 2 points, and 1 point respectively. The bonus for winning in the third round requires a fight be stopped before 4:59 of the third round. No bonus point will be awarded if a fighter wins via decision. For example, if a fighter wins a fight in the first round, then the fighter will receive 6 total points. A decision win will result in three total points.

The two were originally expected to fight on October 4, 2008 at the [[BankAtlantic Center]] in [[Sunrise, Florida]] as part of [[CBS]]' ''Saturday Night Fights'' at [[EliteXC: Heat]]. However, Shamrock received a cut to the left eye during a warm-up only hours before the fight was to take place. That bout was eventually rescheduled for this event. The co-main event was originally expected to feature a [[List of Bellator champions#Featherweight World Championship|Bellator Featherweight Championship]] bout between current champion [[Patrício Freire]] and former [[WSOF Featherweight Championship|WSOF Featherweight champion]] [[Georgi Karakhanyan]]. However, Karakhanyan was forced out of the bout due to an injury and was replaced by Season 10 Featherweight Tournament winner [[Daniel Weichel]]. [[Bobby Lashley]] was originally scheduled to face [[James Thompson (fighter)|James Thompson]] at this event.{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/2015/04/bobby-lashley-vs-james-thompson-rematch-set-for-bellator-138-in-st-louis|title=Bobby Lashley vs. James Thompson rematch set for Bellator 138 in St. Louis|publisher=mmajunkie.com|author=Brent Brookhouse|date=April 13, 2015}} However, on June 10, it was announced that Thompson pulled out of the bout due to injury and was replaced by [[Dan Charles (fighter)|Dan Charles]].{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/2015/06/james-thompson-injured-dan-charles-steps-in-vs-bobby-lashley-at-bellator-138|title=James Thompson injured, Dan Charles steps in vs. Bobby Lashley at Bellator 138|publisher=mmajunkie.com|author=Mike Bohn|date=10 June 2015}} Bellator 138 was the highest rated Bellator event to date before Bellator 149.

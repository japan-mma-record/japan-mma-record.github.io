{{Clear}} ==Standings== The PFL points system is based on results of the match. The winner of a fight receives 3 points. If the fight ends in a draw, both fighters will receive 1 point. A no-contest will be scored as a draw. The bonus for winning a fight in the first, second, or third round is 3 points, 2 points, and 1 point respectively. The bonus for winning in the third round requires a fight be stopped before 4:59 of the third round. No bonus point will be awarded if a fighter wins via decision. For example, if a fighter wins a fight in the first round, then the fighter will receive 6 total points. A decision win will result in three total points. If a fighter misses weight, the opponent (should they comply with weight limits) will receive 3 points due to a walkover victory, regardless of winning or losing the bout, with the fighter who missed weight being deducted 1 standings point; if the non-offending fighter subsequently wins with a stoppage, all bonus points will be awarded. A fighter who was unable to compete for any reason, will receive a 1-point penalty (-1 point in the standings). ===Women's Lightweight=== The women's 2021 roster included the 2019 PFL champion [[Kayla Harrison]], [[Cindy Dandois]], [[Genah Fabian]], Olena Kolesnyk, Mariana Morais, [[Larissa Pacheco]], Julija Pajic and Laura Sanchez. Kaitlin Young and Taylor Guardado rounded out the roster. Pacheco was eliminated at the semifinals when she was two pounds over the lightweight limit.{{cite web | url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/8/18/22631285/larissa-pacheco-out-of-pfl-playoffs-after-missing-weight-mariana-morais-fight-taylor-guardado-pfl-8 | title=Larissa Pacheco out of PFL playoffs after missing weight, Mariana Morais to fight Taylor Guardado at PFL 8 | date=18 August 2021 }}

===Featherweight=== Eight fighters were initially announced for the 2021 season. The two-time PFL champion [[Lance Palmer]], UFC veteran [[Sheymon Moraes]], [[Bellator MMA|Bellator]] veteran [[Bubba Jenkins]], Tyler Diamond, Sung Bin Jo, [[Movlid Khaybulaev]], [[Brendan Loughnane]] and Jason Soares. Chris Wade, who previously competed in the PFL Lightweight division, was added to the featherweight roster. Anthony Dizy was later added as well.{{cite web |last1=Pettry |first1=Jay |title=PFL Completes 60-Fighter Roster for 2021 Season Including Gleison Tibau, Kaitlin Young |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/PFL-Completes-60Fighter-Roster-for-2021-Season-Including-Gleison-Tibau-Kaitlin-Young-177890 |website=sherdog.com |access-date=26 February 2021}} Jason Soares was not medically cleared, hence he was replaced by Lazar Stojadinovic.{{cite web |title=Curtis Millender Joins PFL 2021 Tournament, Faces Rory MacDonald on April 29 |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Curtis-Millender-Joins-PFL-2021-Tournament-Faces-Rory-MacDonald-on-April-29-178679 |website=Sherdog |access-date=9 April 2021}}

CW 93 - Cage Warriors 93 took place on April 28, 2018 at Lisebergshallen, Gothenburg, with 13 fights on the card. The main event featured Carlo Pedersoli Jr. vs Nicolas Dalby in the Welterweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Carlo Pedersoli Jr. defeated Nicolas Dalby by Decision (Split).
  • Soren Bak defeated Alexander Jacobsen by Submission (Arm-Triangle Choke) in round 2.
  • Alan Carlos defeated Norman Paraisy by Decision (Unanimous).

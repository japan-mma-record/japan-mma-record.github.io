Who won ACB 10 - Coliseum Time?

Abdul-Kerim Edilov defeated Cristian Torres by Submission in round 1 (1:43) in the Light Heavyweight title main event of ACB 10 - Coliseum Time, held Saturday, October 4, 2014 at Arena Coliseum, Grozny.

ACB 10 - Coliseum Time was an MMA event from Absolute Championship Akhmat, held Saturday, October 4, 2014 at Arena Coliseum, Grozny, Russia. A championship title was on the line. 14 fights took place on the card.

CW 97 - Cage Warriors 97 took place on September 29, 2018 at Ice Arena Wales, Cardiff, with 21 fights on the card. The main event featured Jack Shore vs Weslley Maia in the Bantamweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Jack Shore defeated Weslley Maia by TKO (Elbows and Punches) in round 1.
  • Mason Jones defeated Kacper Formela by TKO (Knees to the Body) in round 1.
  • Brian Bouland defeated Kris Edwards by Decision (Unanimous).

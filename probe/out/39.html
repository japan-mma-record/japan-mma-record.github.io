{{Short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2023}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 290: Bader vs. Fedor 2 |image=Bellator290.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2023|02|4}} |venue=[[Kia Forum]] |city=[[Inglewood, California]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator MMA vs. Rizin|Bellator MMA vs. Rizin FF]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 291|Bellator 291: Amosov vs. Storley 2]] }} '''''Bellator 290: Bader vs. Fedor 2''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on February 4, 2023, at the [[Kia Forum]] in [[Inglewood, California]], United States. The card aired live on [[CBS]] and [[Paramount Plus]], the first time the promotion has aired live on the network and the first time MMA has been broadcast live on CBS since 2010. == Background == The event marked the promotion's seventh visit to Inglewood and first since [[Bellator 263]] in July 2021. The main event of the Bellator 290 featured MMA veteran and former [[List of Pride Fighting Championships champions#Heavyweight Championship|PRIDE Heavyweight Champion]] [[Fedor Emelianenko]]'s last fight before his retirement. Emelianenko challenged [[Bellator Heavyweight World Championship|Bellator Heavyweight champion]] [[Ryan Bader]] (also former [[Bellator Light Heavyweight World Championship|Bellator Light Heavyweight

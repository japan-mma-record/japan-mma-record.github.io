Who won ACA 89: ACB 93 - Krasnodar?

Ali Bagov defeated Abdul-Aziz Abdulvakhabov by Decision in the Lightweight main event of ACA 89: ACB 93 - Krasnodar, held Saturday, September 8, 2018.

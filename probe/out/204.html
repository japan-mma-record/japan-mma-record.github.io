About the event

CW 101 - Cage Warriors 101 took place on February 16, 2019 at Exhibition Centre, Liverpool, with 17 fights on the card. The main event featured Tom Aspinall vs Sofiane Boukichou in the Heavyweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Tom Aspinall defeated Sofiane Boukichou by TKO in round 1.
  • Tim Barnett defeated Adam Ventre by KO (knee) in round 2.
  • Manuel Bilic defeated Bryan Creighton by technical submission (rear-naked choke) in round 3.

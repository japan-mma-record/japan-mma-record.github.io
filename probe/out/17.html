[[Urijah Faber]] was expected to make his [[Bantamweight (MMA)|bantamweight]] debut against Japanese striker [[Takeya Mizugaki]].{{cite news|url=http://www.mmaweekly.com/absolutenm/templates/dailynews.asp?articleid=11707&zoneid=13|title=Faber moves to 135lbs, faces Mizugaki August 18|publisher=mmaweekly.com|date=June 8, 2010|access-date=July 9, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100713141135/http://www.mmaweekly.com/absolutenm/templates/dailynews.asp?articleid=11707&zoneid=13|archive-date=July 13, 2010|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} However, Faber was forced off the card with an injury. As a result, Mizugaki was also pulled from the event.{{cite news|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/19877/injury-forces-urijah-faber-out-of-wec-50-replacement-opponent-will-not-be-secured.mma|title=Injury forces Urijah Faber out of WEC 50, Takeya Mizugaki pulled from card|publisher=mmajunkie.com|date=July 9, 2010|access-date=July 9, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100712050943/http://mmajunkie.com/news/19877/injury-forces-urijah-faber-out-of-wec-50-replacement-opponent-will-not-be-secured.mma|archive-date=July 12, 2010|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}} The Faber/Mizugaki bout was simply pushed back, to [[WEC 52]].{{cite news|url=http://www.mmaweekly.com/absolutenm/templates/dailynews.asp?articleid=12023&zoneid=13|title=Urijah Faber out of WEC 50 due to injury|publisher=mmajunkie.com|date=July 9, 2010}}{{cite web| url=http://www.mmarising.com/news/2010/07/25/faber-vs-mizugaki-now-targeted-for-the-fall/| title=Faber vs Mizugaki Now Targeted For The Fall| publisher=MMARising.com| date=July 26, 2010}} Faber won the rescheduled matchup by first round submission.

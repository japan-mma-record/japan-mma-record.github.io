Current [[Bellator Light Heavyweight Champion]] [[Vadim Nemkov]] was to defend his title against [[Anthony Johnson (fighter)|Anthony “Rumble” Johnson]], while current [[Bellator Heavyweight Champion]] [[Ryan Bader]] faced [[Corey Anderson (fighter)|Corey Anderson]] in the co-main event.{{Cite web|title=Vadim Nemkov vs. Anthony Johnson, Ryan Bader vs. Corey Anderson set for Oct. 16 in Phoenix|url=https://www.cbssports.com/mma/news/vadim-nemkov-vs-anthony-johnson-ryan-bader-vs-corey-anderson-set-for-oct-16-in-phoenix/|access-date=2021-08-01|website=CBSSports.com|language=en}}{{Cite web|title=BELLATOR MMA LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT WORLD GRAND PRIX RESUMES WITH SEMIFINALS AT FOOTPRINT CENTER IN PHOENIX OCTOBER 16|url=https://www.bellator.com/article/Bellator-MMA-LHWGP-Returns-Footprint-Center|website=Bellator}} On September 18, it was announced that [[Anthony Johnson (fighter)|Anthony Johnson]] was forced to pull out of the bout due to an illness that was to keep him out until 2022. Nemkov instead faced the alternate [[Julius Anglickas]].{{Cite web|date=2021-09-18|title=Anthony Johnson has 'never been this afraid of anything in my life' as non-COVID illness pauses career|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/09/mma-news-anthony-johnson-afraid-illness-dig-deep-non-covid-pause-career-title-fight-vadim-nemkov|access-date=2021-09-18|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}}{{Cite web|last=Anderson|first=Jay|date=2021-09-18|title=Bellator 268: Julius Anglickas Steps in for Rumble, Will Fight Nemkov for Title|url=https://cagesidepress.com/2021/09/18/bellator-268-julius-anglickas-steps-rumble-will-fight-nemkov-title/|access-date=2021-09-19|website=Cageside Press|language=en-US}} Anglickas's opponent [[Karl Albrektsson]] was rescheduled with [[Dovletdzhan Yagshimuradov]].{{Cite web|title=Updates Bellator 268.

Background == In November 2023, it was announced that the PFL had acquired rival promotion [[Bellator MMA]].{{Cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2023/11/pfl-bellator-acquistion-ufc-sale-promotions-merger-future-plans|title=PFL officially announces Bellator acquisition, plans revealed for future|publisher=MMA Junkie|author=Nolan King|date=2023-11-20|access-date=2023-11-20}} Speculation was that the company would run a champion against champion event in 2024, bolstered by appearances of Bellator MMA champions at PFL's final event of 2023.{{cite news |date=20 November 2023 |title=Professional Fighters League acquires rival MMA promotion Bellator in 'transformative deal' |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/mixed-martial-arts/67478710 |access-date=28 September 2026 |work=BBC Sport}} A champion versus champion card was announced on January 16, 2024, and scheduled for the following month. Originally, the event was to see four champions from four divisions (heavyweight, middleweight, welterweight, and light heavyweight) defend their titles from their respective organizations.{{Cite web |date=2024-01-16 |title=Bellator MMA vs. PFL supercard: Four champion vs. champion fights set for February in Saudi Arabia |url=https://www.cbssports.com/mma/news/bellator-mma-vs-pfl-supercard-four-champion-vs-champion-fights-set-for-february-in-saudi-arabia/ |access-date=2024-01-16 |website=CBSSports.com |language=en}} However, two champion versus champion matches were altered leading up to the card: *[[2018 in Professional Fighters League#2018 PFL Welterweight playoffs|2018]] and [[2023 in Professional Fighters League#2023 PFL Welterweight playoffs|2023 PFL welterweight]] winner [[Magomed Magomedkerimov]] was originally announced to face the [[Bellator Welterweight Champion]] (also former [[Legacy Fighting Alliance#LFA Welterweight Championship|LFA Welterweight Champion]]) [[Jason Jackson (fighter)|Jason Jackson]] at this event. However, Magomedkerimov withdrew from the bout due to injury in January 2024 and was replaced by [[2019 in Professional Fighters League#2019 PFL Welterweight playoffs|2019]] and [[2021 in Professional Fighters League#2021 PFL Welterweight playoffs|2021 PFL welterweight]] winner [[Ray Cooper III]] in a 180 pounds catchweight bout.{{Cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2024/01/pfl-vs-bellator-fight-card-ray-cooper-vs-magomed-magomedkerimov-jason-jackson|title=Ray Cooper III replaces Magomed Magomedkerimov at PFL vs. Bellator event, faces Jason Jackson|publisher=MMA Junkie|author=Nolan King|date=2024-01-22|access-date=2024-01-22}} *A featherweight bout between reigning [[Bellator Featherweight Champion]] [[Patrício Pitbull]] and [[2023 in Professional Fighters League#2023 PFL Featherweight playoffs|2023 PFL featherweight]] winner [[Jesus Pinedo]] was scheduled for this event; however, Pinedo pulled out a week before the event with a back injury.{{Cite web |last=Lee |first=Alexander K. |date=2024-02-17 |title=Patricio Pitbull vs. Jesus Pinedo off PFL vs. Bellator card following Pinedo withdrawal Pitbull was instead booked against [[2023 in Professional Fighters League#2023 PFL Featherweight playoffs|2023 PFL featherweight]] finalist Gabriel Alves Braga who was already on the card, while Braga's opponent, [[Aaron Pico]], was booked in a rematch against [[Henry Corrales]].{{Cite web |last=Newswire |first=MMA Fighting |date=2024-02-19 |title=Gabriel Braga vs. Patricio Pitbull set for In addition, the card featured the MMA return of [[Claressa Shields]] and the professional MMA debut of Biaggio Ali Walsh, the grandson of [[Muhammad Ali]] who previously competed for PFL in amateur bouts.

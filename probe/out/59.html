CWFC 84 - Cage Warriors Fighting Championship 84 took place on June 2, 2017 at Indigo at The O2, London, with 10 fights on the card. The main event featured Nathaniel Wood vs Marko Kovacevic in the Bantamweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Nathaniel Wood defeated Marko Kovacevic by KO (Punches) in round 1.
  • Kenneth Bergh defeated Norman Paraisy by Submission (Guillotine Choke) in round 2.
  • Matt Inman defeated Brad Wheeler by Decision (Unanimous).

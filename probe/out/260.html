About the event

CWFC 87 - Cage Warriors Fighting Championship 87 took place on October 14, 2017 at Newport Centre, Newport, with 20 fights on the card. The main event featured Roberto Soldic vs Lewis Long in the Welterweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Roberto Soldic defeated Lewis Long by KO (head kick) in round 1.
  • Craig White defeated Matt Inman by TKO (punches) in round 1.
  • Jack Shore defeated Mattia Galbiati by KO (knee) in round 3.

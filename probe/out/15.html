{{Short description|Mixed martial arts event in 2021}} {{use mdy dates|date=September 2021}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 266: Davis vs. Romero |image=Bellator266.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2021|09|18}} |venue=[[SAP Center]] |city=[[San Jose, California]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator 265|Bellator 265: Kongo vs. Kharitonov]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 267|Bellator 267: Lima vs. MVP 2]] }} '''''Bellator 266: Davis vs. Romero''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on September 18, 2021, at the [[SAP Center]] in [[San Jose, California]], United States.{{Cite web|last=Helwani|first=Ariel|title=Yoel Romero's Bellator debut is set (again)|url=https://arielhelwani.substack.com/p/yoel-romeros-bellator-debut-is-set|access-date=2021-08-13|website=arielhelwani.substack.com|date=August 13, 2021}}{{Cite web|date=2021-08-13|title=Yoel Romero's Bellator debut rebooked vs. Phil Davis on September 18|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/08/bellator-yoel-romero-debut-booked-vs-phil-davis-september-18|access-date=2021-08-13|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} == Background == Former UFC middleweight title contender [[Yoel Romero]] made his Bellator debut against [[Phil Davis (fighter)|Phil Davis]], another former UFC contender, at this event. Romero, 44, was slated to debut for the promotion in the first round of their light heavyweight tournament against [[Anthony Johnson (fighter)|Anthony Johnson]] in May, but was forced to withdraw due to a medical issue concerning his eye.{{Cite web|last=Guillen|first=Adam Jr.|date=2021-08-13|title=Yoel Romero vs Phil Davis headlines Bellator event on Sept. 18 in San event featured a bout between #4 ranked Welterweight [[Neiman Gracie]] and Mark Lemminger.{{Cite web|last=Cruz|first=Guilherme|date=2021-08-26|title=Neiman Gracie vs. Mark Lemminger co-headlines Bellator 266 in San Jose|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/8/26/22643359/neiman-gracie-mark-lemminger-coheadlines-bellator-266-san-jose|access-date=2021-08-26|website=MMA Fighting|language=en}} A lineup|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/07/bellator-263-alejandra-lara-vs-deanna-bennett-womens-flyweight-lineup|access-date=2021-07-04|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} The bout was On August 13, it was announced that the bout was moved once again, this time to this event.{{Cite web|title=Bellator 266: Yoel Romero to make promotional debut against Phil Davis on Sept. 18 card in San Jose|url=https://www.cbssports.com/mma/news/bellator-266-yoel-romero-to-make-promotional-debut-against-phil-davis-on-sept-18-card-in-san-jose/|access-date=2021-08-13|website=CBSSports.com|language=en}} A lightweight bout between [[Adam Piccolotti]] and Saul Rogers was scheduled for this event. However, Piccolotti pulled out of the bout for unknown reasons and was replaced by [[Georgi Karakhanyan]].{{Cite web|date=2021-09-07|title=With Adam Piccolotti out, Georgi Karakhanyan steps in to face Saul Rogers at Bellator 266|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/09/adam-piccolotti-out-georgi-karakhanyan-vs-saul-rogers-bellator-266-mma-fight-booking-matchup|access-date=2021-09-07|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} Bantamweight bouts between Socrates Hernandez and Pedro Juarez and Erik Gunha versus Bobby Soronio III were scheduled for the event; however Juarez and Gunha pulled out their respective bouts so Hernandez and Soronio III instead faced each other.{{Cite web|last=Staff|date=2021-09-17|title=Bellator 266: Davis vs. Romero Weigh-In Results|url=https://cagesidepress.com/2021/09/17/bellator-266-weigh-in/|access-date=2021-09-17|website=Cageside Press|language=en-US}} Eddie Abasolo was scheduled to face Art Hernandez in a 160 pounds catchweight bout, but Hernandez did not make weight. Therefore, Abasolo did not weigh in and the bout was scrapped. At the weigh-ins, [[DeAnna Bennett]] missed weight for her bout. Bennett weighed in at 129.2 pounds, 3.2 pounds over the flyweight non-title fight limit. The bout proceeded at catchweight and Bennett was fined a percentage of her purse which went to her opponent [[Alejandra Lara]]. == Results == {{MMAevent}} {{MMAevent card|Main Card (Showtime)}}

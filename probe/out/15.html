About the event

Deep - Hamamatsu Impact 2026 1st Round took place on May 31, 2026 at Act City Hamamatsu, Hamamatsu, with 16 fights on the card. The main event featured Shinsuke Matsubara vs Takuma Uchiyama in the Bantamweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Shinsuke Matsubara defeated Takuma Uchiyama by KO (punch) in round 1.
  • Takuma Iwase defeated Kentaro Hachisu by TKO (punches) in round 1.
  • Kenichi Kinoshita defeated Atsuki Okuno by disqualification in round 1.

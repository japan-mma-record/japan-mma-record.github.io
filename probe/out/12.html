{{short description|WEC MMA event in 2009}} {{Use mdy dates|date=April 2025}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 45: Cerrone vs. Ratcliff |image= WEC 45 Donald Cerrone vs. Ed Ratcliff 2 poster.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=December 19, 2009 |venue=[[The Pearl at The Palms]] |city=[[Paradise, Nevada]] |attendance=1,741 |gate=$102,700 |buyrat= |previousevent=[[WEC 44|WEC 44: Brown vs. Aldo]] |followingevent= [[WEC 46|WEC 46: Varner vs. Henderson]] }} '''''WEC 45: Cerrone vs. Ratcliff''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] that took place on December 19, 2009 at [[The Pearl at The Palms]] in [[Las Vegas]].{{cite web|url=http://www.wec.tv/index.cfm?fa=news.detail&gid=23483| title=WEC 45:Cerrone vs. Ratcliff}}www.wec.tv/index.cfm It marked the first time the WEC has held back-to-back events at The Pearl at The Palms. The event drew an estimated 330,000 viewers on [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus]].{{Cite web|url=http://www.tapology.com/fightcenter/events/wec-45-cerrone-vs-ratcliff|title=WEC 45: Cerrone vs Ratcliff | MMA Event}} ==Background== [[Damacio Page]] was originally slated to face [[Takeya Mizugaki]] at this event, but was pulled from the card and replaced by [[Scott Jorgensen]].{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/16678/scott-jorgensen-vs-takeya-mizugaki-added-to-wec-45-cerrone-vs-ratcliff.mma|title=Scott Jorgensen vs. Takeya Mizugaki added to "WEC 45: Cerrone vs. Ratcliff"|access-date=2009-10-30|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091103105650/http://mmajunkie.com/news/16678/scott-jorgensen-vs-takeya-mizugaki-added-to-wec-45-cerrone-vs-ratcliff.mma|archive-date=2009-11-03|url-status=dead}} [[Mackens Semerzier]] was once linked to a bout with [[Erik Koch]] at this event, but it was later confirmed that Koch would instead be facing [[Jameel Massouh]].{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/16686/erik-koch-meets-jameel-massouh-not-mackens-semerzier-at-wec-45-in-december.mma|title=Erik Koch meets Jameel Massouh, not Mackens Semerzier, at WEC 45 in December}} Debuting featherweights [[Brandon Visher]] and [[Tyler Toner]] were scheduled to face each other at this event, but Toner was removed from the event and replaced by fellow WEC newcomer [[Courtney Buck]].{{cite web|url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/17226/with-tyler-toner-scratched-brandon-visher-gets-courtney-buck-at-wec-45.mma|title=With Tyler Toner scratched, Brandon Visher gets Courtney Buck at WEC 45 (Updated)|access-date=2009-12-16|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091217163354/http://mmajunkie.com/news/17226/with-tyler-toner-scratched-brandon-visher-gets-courtney-buck-at-wec-45.mma|archive-date=2009-12-17|url-status=dead}} The Visher/Toner bout was rescheduled for [[WEC 48]] the following April, where Toner won via TKO.

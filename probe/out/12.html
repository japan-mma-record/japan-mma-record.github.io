Pitbull is the current [[Bellator Lightweight Champion|Bellator Lightweight]] and [[Bellator Featherweight Champion|Featherweight Champion]], who is currently on a seven-fight winning streak and hasn't lost since August 2016. He defeated [[Emmanuel Sanchez]] via a first-round guillotine choke in their rematch at [[Bellator 255]].

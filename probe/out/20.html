Cyborg completed her third successful title defense since coming over from the [[Ultimate Fighting Championship|UFC]] in 2019. The 36-year-old captured the championship in her first promotional appearance when she stopped [[Julia Budd]] in the fourth round at Bellator 238 in January 2020. Since then, Cyborg has gone on to earn stoppage wins over [[Arlene Blencowe|Arlene Biencowe]] and [[Leslie Smith (fighter)|Leslie Smith]]. Kavanagh got her first shot at Bellator gold in her ninth promotional appearance. The SBG Ireland featherweight entered the bout on back-to-back victories — a unanimous decision win over [[Katharina Lehner]] at Bellator Euro Series 9 in October, and a second-round TKO of Olga Rubin at Bellator 234.

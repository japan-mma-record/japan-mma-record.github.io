The main card of the event, titled "Bellator London: Lima vs. MVP 2," aired live at 4 p.m. ET on Showtime. Page returned to fight in his home city for the first time since 2019, looking to avenge the lone defeat of his professional career when he was knocked out by Lima in the second round of their [[Bellator MMA in 2018#Bellator Welterweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Welterweight World Grand Prix]] semifinal bout two years ago at [[Bellator MMA in 2019#Bellator 221|Bellator 221]].

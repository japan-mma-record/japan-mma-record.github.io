The year 2026 is the tenth year in the history of Legacy Fighting Alliance (LFA) a mixed martial arts promotion based in the United States.[1]

Background

[edit]

On January 7, 2026, it was announced that the promotion has officially parted ways with UFC Fight Pass and signed a new broadcast deal with Vice TV, which will air 25 events in 2026. This marking a significant shift for the regional promotion as the UFC also cut ties with several other regional partners.[2]

On October 20, 2026, it was announced a multi-year partnership deal with FloSports for North American streaming, which will begin LFA 244 to events in December 2026, followed the 25 events in 2027.[3]

LFA 248: Costa vs. Hartshorn is an upcoming mixed martial arts event promoted by Legacy Fighting Alliance that is scheduled to take place on December 18, 2026, in Paradise, Nevada, part of the Las Vegas Valley, United States.

Background

[edit]

The event will mark the promotion's third visit to Las Vegas and first since LFA 203 in March 2025.[53]

A LFA Flyweight Championship bout for the vacant title between Davi Costa and Mason Carzino-Hartshorn is scheduled to headline the event.[54]

A LFA Light Heavyweight Championship bout for the vacant title between Shealor Ladd and Wildemar Santos is scheduled to serve as the co-main event.[55]

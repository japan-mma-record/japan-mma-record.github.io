The event took place on Wednesday, September 5, 2007 at the [[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] in [[Las Vegas, Nevada]] and aired live on the [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus Network]].{{cite web\n| url=http://www.nokaut.com/?id=12&solo_news=984&lang=e&cur=eur\n| title=WEC 30 Takes Shape\n| last=Bolduc\n| first=Justin\n| date=August 10, 2007\n| access-date=2007-08-10\n| publisher=Nokaut\n}}\n\n\nThe event featured a [[Lightweight (MMA)|lightweight]] title match between champion [[Rob McCullough|\"Razor\" Rob McCullough]] and [[Richard Crunkilton]]. Additionally, [[Chase Beebe]] defended his [[Bantamweight (MMA)|bantamweight]] title against [[Brazilian jiu-jitsu]] expert [[Rani Yahya]], who replaced [[Manny Tapia]] on the card after Tapia suffered a knee injury.\n\nFormer [[Ultimate Fighting Championship|UFC]] [[UFC Lightweight Championship|Lightweight Champion]] [[Jens Pulver]] was scheduled to make his WEC & featherweight debut at this event against [[Cub Swanson]], but was pulled from the card due to a knee injury. The matchup was rescheduled for [[WEC 31]] three months later, where Pulver won by submission.{{cite web\n| url=http://www.thefightnetwork.com/news_detail.php?nid=4330\n| title=PM Update – July 17\n|

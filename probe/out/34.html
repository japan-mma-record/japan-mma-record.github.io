Bellator held '''18 events''' in 2021. ==Background== The promotion went on a hiatus following Bellator 254 on December 10, 2020, which would be the final card to air on [[CBS Sports Network]].{{cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/2/3/22265308/bellator-targets-april-9-for-2021-return-in-familiar-venue|title=Bellator targets April 9 for 2021 return in familiar venue|author=Steven Marrocco|date=February 3, 2021|publisher=mmafighting.com}} Beginning in April, Bellator's U.S broadcasts moved to the [[Pay television|premium]] [[Showtime (TV network)|Showtime]] network.{{cite web|url=https://www.cbssports.com/mma/news/bellator-set-for-showtime-debut-in-april-as-star-studded-light-heavyweight-world-grand-prix-headlines-slate/amp/|title=Bellator set for Showtime debut in April as star-studded Light Heavyweight World Grand Prix headlines slate|work=CBS Sports|author=Brent Brookhouse|date=2021-02-09|accessdate=2021-02-09}} Bellator 255, which took place on April 2, 2021, was the promotion's debut on Showtime, and the first MMA card to air on the network in over eight years; the last event being [[Strikeforce: Marquardt vs. Saffiedine]] on January 12, 2013.

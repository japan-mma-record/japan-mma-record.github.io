About the event

CWFC 89 - Cage Warriors Fighting Championship 89 took place on November 25, 2017 at Lotto Arena, Antwerp, with 21 fights on the card. The main event featured Dominique Steele vs Karl Amoussou in the Catchweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Dominique Steele defeated Karl Amoussou by unanimous decision.
  • Cindy Dandois defeated Kerry Hughes by TKO (punches) in round 1.
  • Mauro Cerilli defeated Nills van Noord by TKO (punches) in round 1.

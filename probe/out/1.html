As part of the $1 million [[2023 in Bellator MMA#Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix]], a [[Bellator Lightweight World Championship]] bout between current champion [[Usman Nurmagomedov]] and [[Benson Henderson]] (former [[List of World Extreme Cagefighting champions#Lightweight Championship|WEC]] and [[List of UFC champions#Lightweight Championship|UFC Lightweight Champion]]) headlined the event. In the co-main event, [[Tofiq Musayev]] faced off against [[Alexandr Shabliy]] in a quarterfinal match.

OKTAGON 84: PARADEISER vs. BRITO

14.02.2026, 17:00 - OSTRAVAR ARÉNA, Ostrava-město

KOZMA IS BACK!

After two long years, OKTAGON storms back into the toughest city in the country!

The grand return of Kozma and OKTAGON. After an intense 26-month journey into new cities, the promotion’s elite fighters are heading back home to Ostrava, where fighting blood runs through the locals’ veins. On February 14, 2026, the best and loudest fans on the planet will witness an all-out MMA spectacle at Ostravar Arena.

A championship firefight for the welterweight crown. Division #1, world #63 and former lightweight champion Rony Paradeiser is determined to conquer the welterweight class. Standing in his way is former welterweight champion Kaik Brito, who boasts all 18 victories before the limit and became OKTAGON king right here in Ostrava three years ago. Can we expect a déjà vu?

After a year away, the cage welcomes back one of the greatest figures in OKTAGON history – six-time former welterweight champion David Kozma. His battles are etched among the organization’s finest. Across from him stands Slovak standout and champion of a domestic organization, Jozef Wittner, making his OKTAGON return after five years.

The biggest homegrown talent, 22-year-old Batfalský, has one goal only: victory in Ostrava. But the two-time German-organization champion Black-Dell is coming for his head.

A collision of two elite prospects from their respective countries. The unstoppable “Ostrava Express” Mudroch, with all 8 wins coming in the opening round and riding a six-fight streak, looks to run over Serbian powerhouse Fedor Duric, who has won 8 of his last 9 fights.

Local fan favorite and hardened warrior Jan Široký returns to OKTAGON after two years, facing the brutal firepower of longtime veteran Fodor.

Battle-tested Kuzník, shaped by the toughest wars, meets the razor-sharp grappling specialist Nafuka. Who will push harder for victory?

Also on the fight card: explosive MMA prospect and amateur Czech national champion Klimša, plus the surging, versatile Olomouc fighter Petr Bartoněk, who earned his OKTAGON contract through PML.

An unbelievably stacked event! Come to Ostravar Arena on February 14, 2026, and we guarantee you’ll get goosebumps. Tickets are on sale now.

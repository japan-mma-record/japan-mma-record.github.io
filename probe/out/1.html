[[Helena Crevar]] (openweight) | mostwins = | mostrecent = }} The '''Craig Jones Invitational''' ('''CJI''') is an international [[Submission wrestling|submission grappling]] tournament organized by [[Craig Jones (grappler)|Craig Jones]]. The inaugural tournament was held August 16–17, 2024 at the [[Thomas & Mack Center]] in [[Las Vegas]], Nevada in the United States.{{cite web | title=Dates And Location Announced For Inaugural Craig Jones Invitational | website=jitsmagazine | date=2024-06-01 | url=https://jitsmagazine.com/dates-and-location-announced-for-inaugural-craig-jones-invitational/}}[https://mmauncensored.com/list-competitors-cji-craig-jones-invitational/ BJJ stars leave ADCC 2024 for $1M CJI prize] ''MMA Uncensored'', Anton Tabuena, May 30, 2024 The event was streamed live on [[YouTube]] for free.{{cite web | title=Craig Jones announces $2 million tournament on same weekend as ADCC | website=grapplinginsider | date=2024-05-24 | url=https://grapplinginsider.com/craig-jones-announces-2-million-tournament-on-same-weekend-as-adcc/}} Craig Jones defeated [[Gabi Garcia]] in an intergender superfight, [[Nick Rodriguez]] won the 80kg+ division tournament, and [[Kade Ruotolo]] won the −80kg division tournament. The tournament winners in each weight division received $1 million prize money. The Craig Jones Invitational 2 (CJI 2) was held August 30–31, 2025, returning to the Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas.{{cite web |last1=Jones |first1=Phil |title=Craig Jones Announces CJI 2 Confirmed For 2025 After Funding Secured |url=https://jitsmagazine.com/craig-jones-announces-cji-2-confirmed-for-2025-after-funding-secured/ |website=Jits Magazine |access-date=12 December 2024}} CJI 2 featured a $1 million team-based grappling tournament won by [[B Team Jiu Jitsu|The B-Team]], an [[openweight]] $100,000 women's tournament won by [[Helena Crevar]], and a non-tournament superfight which saw Craig Jones defeat [[Chael Sonnen]]. The B-Team and [[New Wave Jiu Jitsu|Team New Wave]] initially fought to a draw in the team tournament final before The B-Team were declared the winners by decision; after controversy over the decision, both teams were to be awarded $1 million but Team New Wave were ultimately not given the prize money.{{cite web |last1=Gilpin |first1=Joe |title=CJI 2 Donor Rescinds $1 Million Offer To New Wave|url=https://www.flograppling.com/articles/14527430-cji-2-donor-rescinds-1-million-offer-to-new-wave|work=FloGrappling|access-date=9 September 2025}} ==2024== === Background === [[Craig Jones (grappler)|Craig Jones]] from [[B Team Jiu Jitsu|B-Team Jiu-Jitsu]] is a two-time [[ADCC Submission Fighting World Championship|ADCC]] silver medalist. Disappointed with athlete pay at ADCC, he decided to host a tournament in which all invited athletes receive a higher minimum payout ($10,001) than the male winner of ADCC ($10,000).[https://jacobin.com/2024/06/jiu-jitsu-grappling-exploitation-unionization Jiu Jitsu Fighters Are Grappling With Exploitation] ''Jacobin'', Jack Bedrosian, June 2024 The two men's divisions scheduled for the 2024 tournament were under 80kg and over 80kg, and the winner of each bracket was to be awarded $1 million. Each men's division bracket had 16 competitors. Each participant received $10,001 for competing.{{cite web | title=Legendary Jiu-jitsu Star Craig Jones Waging War On ADCC With Competing Invitational Tournament | website=deadspin | date=2024-06-01 | url=https://deadspin.com/legendary-jiu-jitsu-star-craig-jones-waging-war-on-adcc/}} On ''JRE MMA Show No. 157'', an episode of [[Joe Rogan]]'s podcast, Jones brought $1 million in cash to promote the tournament.{{cite web | title=Joe Rogan podcast guest flashes $1 million cash during interview to promote Jiu-Jitsu tournament: video | website=nypost | date=2024-06-01 | url=https://nypost.com/2024/05/29/us-news/joe-rogan-podcast-guest-craig-jones-brings-1-million-cash-to-interview/ }} A best submission bonus of $50,000 was announced.{{cite web | title=Craig Jones Invitational Announces $50,000 'Best Submission' Bonus | website=jitsmagazine | date=2024-06-23 | url=https://jitsmagazine.com/craig-jones-invitational-announces-50000-best-submission-bonus/}} The dates and location were selected to overlap with [[2024_ADCC_World_Championship|ADCC 2024]], which was held August 17–18, 2024 in Las Vegas. Separate from the men's divisions, Craig Jones was scheduled to face [[Gabi Garcia]] in an intergender superfight, and [[Ffion Davies]] was scheduled to fight [[Mackenzie Dern]] in the women's superfight. [[Kade Ruotolo]] vs. Andrew Tackett from the event's −80kg tournament won Jits Magazine's Match of the Year award.{{cite web |title=The Jits Magazine 2024 BJJ Awards |url=https://jitsmagazine.com/the-jits-magazine-2024-bjj-awards/ |website=Jits Magazine |access-date=24 January 2025}} ==2025== ===Background=== On December 8, 2024, Craig Jones announced a second Craig Jones Invitational tournament to be held in August 2025, with the 2025 tournament adopting a [[Quintet (grappling)|Quintet]]-inspired team format. The event streamed live for free on the [[FloSports|FloGrappling]] [[YouTube]] channel. The Craig Jones Invitational 2 (CJI 2) was held August 30-31, 2025 at the Thomas & Mack Center in Las Vegas. CJI 2 was a two-day event, featuring an eight team tournament, an [[openweight]] women's tournament, and a superfight between Jones and [[Chael Sonnen]] (replacing an injured [[Gable Steveson]]). A total of 46 athletes were scheduled to compete at the event. Teams in the $1 million team-based grappling tournament consisted of five grapplers, each representing a different weight class. Eight teams of five athletes competed; participating teams were: [[10th Planet Jiu Jitsu|Team 10th Planet Jiu Jitsu]], [[B Team Jiu Jitsu|The B-Team]], [[New Wave Jiu Jitsu|Team New Wave]], [[Atos Jiu-Jitsu|Team Atos]], Team Europe, Team Pedigo Submission Fighting, Team Americas, and Team Australasia. The $100,000 four-woman tournament featured: [[Helena Crevar]] ([[Polaris Pro Grappling|Polaris]] and [[Who's Number One|WNO]] Champion), [[Adele Fornarino]] ([[2024 ADCC]] double gold medalist), [[Ana Carolina Vieira (fighter)|Ana Vieira]] (2024 ADCC Champion, 6x [[IBJJF World]] Champ), and [[Sarah Galvão]] (2025 IBJJF Grand Slam Winner).{{cite news |last1=Gilpin |first1=Joe |title=A Full Running List Of Teams & Grapplers For The Craig Jones Invitational 2 |url=https://www.flograppling.com/articles/14068354-a-full-running-list-of-teams-grapplers-for-the-craig-jones-invitational-2 |work=Flograppling |date=July 22, 2025}} Gable Steveson later claimed that he withdrew from the event not because of an injury but rather because Jones asked him to [[match fixing|throw]] their superfight.

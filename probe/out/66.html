Who won OKTAGON 34: Vémola vs Ilič?

Karlos Vemola defeated Aleksandar Ilič by Submission in round 1 (3:52) in the Light Heavyweight title main event of OKTAGON 34: Vémola vs Ilič, held Saturday, July 23, 2022 at Štvanice, Prague.

OKTAGON 34: Vémola vs Ilič was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, July 23, 2022 at Štvanice, Prague, Czechia. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. A championship title was on the line. 34 is part of OKTAGON's numbered event series. 12 fights took place on the card.

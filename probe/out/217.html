About the event

CW 115 - Cage Warriors 115 took place on September 25, 2020 at BEC Arena, Manchester, with 5 fights on the card. The main event featured Jack Cartwright vs Gerardo Fanny in the Lightweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Jack Cartwright defeated Gerardo Fanny by submission (arm-triangle choke) in round 1.
  • James Webb defeated Craig White by submission (arm-triangle choke) in round 1.
  • Jack Grant defeated Yassine Belhadj by submission (guillotine choke) in round 1.

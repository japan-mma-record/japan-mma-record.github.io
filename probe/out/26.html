CW 133 - Cage Warriors 133 took place on March 4, 2022 at Del Mar Arena, San Diego, with 12 fights on the card. The main event featured Kyle Driscoll vs Joshua Jones in the Lightweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Kyle Driscoll defeated Joshua Jones by Decision (Unanimous).
  • Wilson Reis defeated Jeremiah Labiano by Decision (Split).
  • Damon Wilson defeated Ryan Fillingame by TKO (Punches) in round 1.

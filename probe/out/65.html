Who won OKTAGON 33: Buchinger vs Keita?

Losene Keita defeated Ivan Buchinger by TKO in round 1 (3:00) in the Lightweight main event of OKTAGON 33: Buchinger vs Keita, held Saturday, June 4, 2022 at Festhalle Messe Frankfurt, Frankfurt.

OKTAGON 33: Buchinger vs Keita was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, June 4, 2022 at Festhalle Messe Frankfurt, Frankfurt, Germany. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. 33 is part of OKTAGON's numbered event series. 13 fights took place on the card.

About the event

Fighting Nexus - Ready To Go in Nagano 2024 took place on March 10, 2024 at Nakano City Community Sports Center, Nakano, with 20 fights on the card. The main event featured Naohiro Matsumoto vs Ryosuke Takasugi in the Catchweight · 137 lb division.

Top fight results:

  • Naohiro Matsumoto defeated Ryosuke Takasugi by majority decision.
  • Goal Makino defeated Kaito Kiyosawa by submission (rear-naked choke) in round 2.
  • Keisuke Koiso defeated Kengo Akaishi by submission (triangle armbar) in round 1.

{{short description|WEC MMA event in 2008}} {{Use mdy dates|date=April 2025}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 37: Torres vs. Tapia |image=WEC37poster-1.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=December 3, 2008 |venue=[[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] |city=[[Las Vegas, Nevada]] |attendance=643 |gate=$90,125 |buyrate= |previousevent=[[WEC 36|WEC 36: Faber vs. Brown]] |followingevent=[[WEC 38|WEC 38: Varner vs. Cerrone]] }} '''''WEC 37: Torres vs. Tapia''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] on December 3, 2008, at the [[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] in [[Las Vegas, Nevada]]. The event aired live on the [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus Network]]. ==Background== Former [[International Fight League|IFL]] standout Wagnney Fabiano made his WEC debut on this card. A welterweight contest between [[Blas Avena]] and the returning [[Kevin Knabjian]] was originally scheduled for the undercard of this event, but it was later cancelled.{{cite web |url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/13275/wec-37-televised-card-announced.mma |title=WEC 37 televised card announced {{!}} MMAjunkie.com |website=mmajunkie.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090103231229/http://mmajunkie.com/news/13275/wec-37-televised-card-announced.mma |archive-date=2009-01-03}} The [[Mark Muñoz]]-Ricardo Barros fight was the final light heavyweight bout to be put on by the WEC. The division was absorbed into the UFC following this event.

Who won OKTAGON 35: Kincl vs Lohoré?

OKTAGON 35: Kincl vs Lohoré was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, September 17, 2022 at Winning Group Arena, Brno, Czechia. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. A championship title was on the line. 35 is part of OKTAGON's numbered event series. 12 fights took place on the card.

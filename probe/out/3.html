From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of events held and scheduled by K-1, a Japanese kickboxing promotion. The first event, K-1 Sanctuary I, took place on March 30, 1993, at Kōrakuen Hall in Tokyo, Japan.

From 2003, K-1 events were organized by the Fighting and Entertainment Group (FEG). Following FEG's bankruptcy, rights to the K-1 brand were ultimately subsumed by the Hong Kong-based K-1 Global Holdings Limited (K-1 GHL) in 2012.

In 2014, M-1 Sports Media (M-1) acquired rights to use the K-1 brand exclusively in Japan, and has since been producing events for the region under the "K-1 World GP" name. M-1 would ultimately acquire worldwide rights to the K-1 brand in 2023. K-1 branded events held outside Japan (such as the K-1 Fighting Network) are co-promoted shows that weren't produced by M-1.

This list does not include Krush, Krush-EX, Khaos, or K-1 Amateur events.

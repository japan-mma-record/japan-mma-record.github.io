ACA 135: Gasanov vs. Dzhanaev

[edit]
ACA 135: Gasanov vs. Dzhanaev
PromotionAbsolute Championship Akhmat
DateJanuary 28, 2022
VenueSports Hall Coliseum
City Grozny, Russia

Absolute Championship Akhmat 135: Gasanov vs. Dzhanaev was a mixed martial arts event held by Absolute Championship Akhmat on January 28, 2022 at the Sports Hall Coliseum in Grozny, Russia.

Background

[edit]

The event featured a title fight for the ACA Middleweight Championship between the champion Magomedrasul Gasanov and #3 ranked Abdul-Rahman Dzhanaev as the event headliner.

A match for the vacant ACA Flyweight Championship between #2 ranked Murad Zeynulabidov and #4 ranked Imran Bukuev was planned as the event co-headliner.[2] However, has Zeynulabidov to withdraw from the fight due to an injury suffered during his training camp. the bout was canceled.[3]

Bonus awards:

The following fighters were awarded bonuses:

  • $50,000 Performance of the Night: Magomedrasul Gasanov
  • $25,000 Performance of the Night: Abdul-Rakhman Dzhanaev
  • $5000 Stoppage Victory Bonuses: Tomáš Deák, Shamil Shakhbulatov

ACA 136: Bukuev vs Akopyan

[edit]
ACA 136: Bukuev vs Akopyan
PromotionAbsolute Championship Akhmat
DateFebruary 26, 2022
City Moscow, Russia

Absolute Championship Akhmat 136: Bukuev vs Akopyan was a mixed martial arts event held by Absolute Championship Akhmat on February 26, 2022, in Moscow, Russia.

Background

[edit]

The main event was set to feature a welterweight title fight, the reigning ACA Welterweight Champion Abubakar Vagaev was set to defend his title against Ustarmagomed Gadzhidaudov. However, Vagaev has to withdraw on January 26 due to COVID-19.[4] The bout between Imran Bukuev and Aren Akopyan was prompted to the main event of the evening, the fight was for the vacant ACA Flyweight championship. The belt was vacated when Azamat Kerefov officially announced on October 3, 2021, that he voluntarily vacated the title to try free agency.[5]

A welterweight bout between Vitaliy Slipenko and Stefan Sekulić was scheduled as the co-main event. Sekulić stepped in as a replacement for Mark Hulme, who withdrew from the bout.[6]

Bonus awards:

The following fighters were awarded bonuses:

  • $50,000 Performance of the Night: Imran Bukuev
  • $5000 Stoppage Victory Bonuses: Vitaliy Slipenko

officially acquires Bellator MMA, reveals plans for champs vs. champs card in 2024|publisher=mmafighting.com}} == Background == The event marked the promotion's sixth visit to Chicago and the second in 2023 since [[Bellator 297]] in June. A [[List of Bellator champions#Welterweight World Championship|Bellator Welterweight World Championship]] bout between current champion [[Yaroslav Amosov]] and former [[Legacy Fighting Alliance#LFA Welterweight Championship|LFA Welterweight Champion]] [[Jason Jackson (fighter)|Jason Jackson]] headlined the event. A [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Bantamweight World Championship|Bellator Bantamweight World Championship]] unification bout between current champion [[Sergio Pettis]] and interim champion [[Patchy Mix]] (also [[2023 in Bellator MMA#Bellator Bantamweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Bantamweight World Grand Prix]] winner) served as the co-main event. A [[2023 in Bellator MMA#Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix Tournament|Bellator Lightweight World Grand Prix]] semifinal bout between former champion [[Patricky Pitbull]] and [[Alexandr Shabliy]] took place at the event. A bantamweight rematch between [[Raufeon Stots]] and [[Danny Sabatello]] also took place at the event. The pairing previously met at [[Bellator 289]] in December 2022, which Stots won via split decision.{{Cite web|url=https://www.espn.com.au/mma/story/_/id/35222197/raufeon-stots-edges-danny-sabatello-gets-patchy-mix-bellator-final?platform=amp|title=Raufeon Stots edges Danny Sabatello, gets Patchy Mix in Bellator final|publisher=ESPN|author=Jeff Wagenheim|date=2022-12-10|access-date=2023-09-14}} The week of the event, the women's flyweight bout between [[Juliana Velasquez]] and Paula Cristina and a heavyweight bout between [[Daniel James (fighter)|Daniel James]] and [[Ali Isaev]] were cancelled.{{Cite web |last=Guillen |first=Adam |date=2023-11-13 |title=Bellator 301 loses not one, but two fights on the same day |url=https://www.mmamania.com/2023/11/13/23959844/bellator-301-loses-two-fights-on-the-same-day-velasquez-james-pitbull-mckee-amosov-mma |access-date=2023-11-14 |website=MMAmania.com |language=en}} At weigh ins, Keri Taylor-Melendez weighed in at 126.6 pounds, .6 pounds over the flyweight limit.

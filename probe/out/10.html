The event took place on Wednesday, December 12, 2007, at the [[Hard Rock Hotel and Casino (Las Vegas)|Hard Rock Hotel and Casino]] in [[Las Vegas Valley|Las Vegas]], [[Nevada]] and was aired live on the [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus Network]].\n\nThe main event featured a [[Featherweight (MMA)|featherweight]] title match between champion [[Urijah Faber]] and challenger [[Jeff Curran]].{{cite web |url=http://www.wec.tv/index.cfm?fa=eventDetail.fightCard&eid=913 |title=WEC 31 Fight Card}} The card also featured [[Doug Marshall]] defending his [[Light heavyweight (MMA)|light heavyweight]] championship against Ariel Gandulla, Paulo Filho defending his [[Middleweight (MMA)|middleweight]] championship against Chael Sonnen, and the WEC debut of former UFC Lightweight champion [[Jens Pulver]].\n\nFuture WEC & [[Ultimate Fighting Championship|UFC]] champion [[Dominick Cruz]] was scheduled to make his bantamweight debut against [[Charlie Valencia]] at this event but was later forced from the card and replaced by [[Ian McCall (fighter)|Ian McCall]].{{cite web |url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/3263/john-alessio-vs-todd-moore-completes-wec-31-card.mma |title=John Alessio vs. Todd Moore Completes WEC 31 Card - MMAjunkie.com |website=mmajunkie.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080316154103/http://mmajunkie.com/news/3263/john-alessio-vs-todd-moore-completes-wec-31-card.mma |archive-date=2008-03-16}} The Cruz/Valencia bout was rescheduled for [[WEC 34]] the following June, where Cruz won by unanimous decision.\n\nA featherweight bout between Chance Farrar and Micah Miller was originally slated for this event but was pulled from the card three weeks beforehand after Farrar suffered a hand injury.{{cite web |url=http://www.mmaplayground.com/forums/topic12635-1.html |title=Farrar-Miller Postponed for WEC 31}} The bout was instead rescheduled for [[WEC 32]] two months later, where Miller won by knockout.\n\nThe event drew an estimated 629,000 viewers on [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus]].{{cite web | url=http://www.tapology.com/fightcenter/events/wec-31-faber-vs-curran | title=WEC 31: Faber vs Curran | MMA Event }}\n\n==Results==\n{{MMAevent}}\n{{MMAevent card|Main card}}\n{{MMAevent bout|Featherweight|[[Urijah Faber]] (c)|def|[[Jeff Curran]]|Submission (guillotine choke)|2|4:34|For the [[List of WEC champions#Featherweight|Featherweight Championship]].\n}}\n{{MMAevent bout|Middleweight|[[Paulo Filho]] (c)|def|[[Chael Sonnen]]|Submission (armbar)|2|4:55|For the [[List of WEC champions#Middleweight|Middleweight Championship]].

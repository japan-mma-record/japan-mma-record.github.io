About the event

Fighting Nexus - Ready To Go in Nagano 2025 took place on July 6, 2025 at Nakano City Community Sports Center, Nakano, with 3 fights on the card. The main event featured Yuta Miyazawa vs Riku Manabe in the Flyweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Yuta Miyazawa defeated Riku Manabe by unanimous decision.
  • Tomoki Hasegawa defeated Hiroki Imai by unanimous decision.
  • Suzu Ooi defeated An Machiya by submission in round 2.

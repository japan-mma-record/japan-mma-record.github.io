A [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Light Heavyweight World Championship|Bellator Light Heavyweight Championship]] bout between current champion [[Vadim Nemkov]] and [[Yoel Romero]] headlined the event. They were previously expected to meet at [[Bellator 290]] but Nemkov withdrew due to undisclosed reasons.{{Cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/12/27/23527933/vadim-nemkov-yoel-romero-off-bellator-290-johnny-eblen-vs-anatoly-tokov-added-as-new-co-main-event|title=Vadim Nemkov vs. Yoel Romero off Bellator 290, Johnny Eblen vs. Anatoly Tokov added as new co-main event|website=MMAFighting.com|author=Alexander K. champions#Lightweight World Championship|Bellator Lightweight Champion]]) took place.{{Cite simultaneous featherweight and lightweight champion).{{Cite a title in different divisions (after [[Joe Warren (fighter)|Joe Warren]] at [[2014 in Bellator MMA#Bellator 128|Bellator 128]], [[Ryan Bader]] at [[2019 in Bellator MMA#Bellator 214|Bellator 214]],

{{short description|World Extreme Cagefighting mixed martial arts event in 2009}} {{Infobox MMA event |name= WEC 40: Torres vs. Mizugaki |image=WEC40poster2.jpg |promotion=[[World Extreme Cagefighting]] |date=April 5, 2009 |venue= [[UIC Pavilion]] |city=[[Chicago, Illinois|Chicago]], Illinois |attendance=5,257 |gate= |buyrate= |previousevent=[[WEC 39|WEC 39: Brown vs. Garcia]] |followingevent= [[WEC 41|WEC 41: Brown vs. Faber 2]] }} '''''WEC 40: Torres vs. Mizugaki''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event held by [[World Extreme Cagefighting]] on April 5, 2009 at the [[UIC Pavilion]] in [[Chicago, Illinois|Chicago]], Illinois.{{cite news |last=Morgan |first=John |url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/13966/wagnney-fabiano-to-meet-fredson-paixao-not-urijah-faber-at-wec-40.mma |title=WEC 40 to be held in Chicago; headlined by Torres vs. Bowles |work=mmajunkie.com |date=February 10, 2009 |archive-date=February 13, 2009 |access-date=February 10, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090213071816/http://mmajunkie.com/news/13966/wagnney-fabiano-to-meet-fredson-paixao-not-urijah-faber-at-wec-40.mma |url-status=dead }} The event aired live on the [[Versus (TV channel)|Versus Network]]. ==Background== This was the first WEC event to exclusively feature fights in the lightweight, featherweight, and bantamweight weight classes. The organization's heavier weight classes had been folded into the UFC over the previous four months. World Extreme Cagefighting would maintain this weight class structure for the remainder of the promotion's existence. WEC Bantamweight Champion [[Miguel Torres (fighter)|Miguel Torres]] was to defend his title against [[Brian Bowles (fighter)|Brian Bowles]] at this event, but Bowles was forced to withdraw from the bout due to a back injury, and was replaced by WEC newcomer [[Takeya Mizugaki]].{{cite web |url=http://www.mmaweekly.com/absolutenm/templates/dailynews.asp?articleid=8257&zoneid=2 |title=- BOWLES OUT; MIZUGAKI TO FACE TORRES AT WEC 40 - MMA WEEKLY - Mixed Martial Arts & UFC News, Photos, Rankings & more |website=www.mmaweekly.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090228041811/http://www.mmaweekly.com/absolutenm/templates/dailynews.asp?articleid=8257&zoneid=2 |archive-date=2009-02-28}} The Torres/Bowles bout was rescheduled for [[WEC 42]] that August, where Bowles won the title by knockout. Early reports suggested that [[Wagnney Fabiano]] could face former WEC Featherweight Champion [[Urijah Faber]] at this event, though he was later confirmed to be facing [[Fredson Paixao]] instead.{{cite web |url=http://mmajunkie.com/news/13966/wagnney-fabiano-to-meet-fredson-paixao-not-urijah-faber-at-wec-40.mma |title=Wagnney Fabiano to meet Fredson Paixao, not Urijah Faber, at WEC 40 {{!}} MMAjunkie.com |website=mmajunkie.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090213071816/http://mmajunkie.com/news/13966/wagnney-fabiano-to-meet-fredson-paixao-not-urijah-faber-at-wec-40.mma |archive-date=2009-02-13}} [[Anthony Pettis]] was scheduled to make his WEC debut at this event against [[Anthony Njokuani]], but was forced from the card with a broken hand and was replaced by [[Bart Palaszewski]].

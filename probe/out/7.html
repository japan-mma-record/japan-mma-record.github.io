CW 114 - Cage Warriors 114 took place on September 24, 2020 at BEC Arena, Manchester, with 9 fights on the card. The main event featured Luke Shanks vs Samir Faiddine in the Flyweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Luke Shanks defeated Samir Faiddine by Decision (Unanimous).

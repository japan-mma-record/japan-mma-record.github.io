{{short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2023}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 298: Storley vs. Ward |image=Bellator298.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2023|08|11}} |venue=[[Sanford Pentagon]] |city=[[Sioux Falls, South Dakota]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator MMA x Rizin 2]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 299|Bellator 299: Eblen vs. Edwards]] }} '''''Bellator 298: Storley vs. Ward''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on August 11, 2023, at the [[Sanford Pentagon]] in [[Sioux Falls, South Dakota]], United States.{{Cite web |last=Sherdog.com |title=Logan Storley, Brennan Ward to Headline Bellator 298 in South Dakota on Aug. 11 |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Logan-Storley-Brennan-Ward-to-Headline-Bellator-298-in-South-Dakota-on-Aug-11-190053 |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=Sherdog |language=en}} == Background == The event marked the promotion's fourth visit to [[Sioux Falls, South Dakota|Sioux Falls]] and first since [[Bellator 284]] in August 2022.{{Cite web |last=Sherdog.com |title=Bellator Returns to South Dakota on Aug. 11 With Storley vs. Ward Headliner |url=https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Bellator-Returns-to-South-Dakota-on-Aug-11-With-Storley-vs-Ward-Headliner-190030 |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=Sherdog |language=en}} A welterweight bout between former interim [[Bellator Welterweight Champion]] [[Logan Storley]] and [[Brennan Ward]] headlined the event.{{cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/5/24/23735989/james-gallagher-returns-at-bellator-298-faces-cris-lencioni|title=James Gallagher returns at Bellator 298, faces Cris Lencioni|publisher=MMAFighting|date=2023-05-24|access-date=2023-05-25}} The event also features a heavyweight bout between former Interim Heavyweight Champion [[Valentin Moldavsky]] and Steve Mowry. The pairing previously met at Bellator 284 in August 2022, where the bout ended in a no contest as a result of an accidental eye poke by Moldavsky during the first round.{{cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/8/12/23303814/bellator-284-results-goiti-yamauchi-brings-big-drama-show-knocks-out-neiman-gracie-in-second|title=Bellator 284 results: Goiti Yamauchi brings 'Big Drama Show,' knocks out Neiman Gracie in second|publisher=MMA Fighting|author=Steven Marrocco|date=2022-08-12|access-date=2023-05-25}}{{Cite web |title=Bellator MMA {{!}} FIVE FIGHTS ANNOUNCED FOR BRAND NEW BELLATOR MMA EVENT ON AUGUST 11 |url=https://www.bellator.com/article/Five-Fights-Announced-Bellator298-August-11 |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=www.bellator.com |language=en-US}} A bantamweight bout between James Gallagher and Cris Lencioni was scheduled to take place at the event.{{cite web|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2023/05/bellator-298-james-gallagher-vs-cris-lencioni-featherweight-mma-fight-booking-announcement-matchup-sioux-falls|title=James Gallagher returns against Cris Lencioni at Bellator 298 on Aug. 11|publisher=MMAJunkie|author=Farah Hannoun|date=2023-05-24|access-date=2023-05-25}} However after Lencioni suffered cardiac arrest in mid June, he was replaced by James Gonzalez.{{Cite web |last=Heck |first=Mike |date=2023-06-26 |title=James Gallagher now faces James Gonzalez at Bellator 298 |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2023/6/26/23774575/james-gallagher-now-faces-james-gonzalez-at-bellator-298 |access-date=2023-06-27 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}} At weigh-ins, [[Diana Avsaragova]] weighed in at 127.2 pounds, 1.2 pounds over the Flyweight non-title limit.

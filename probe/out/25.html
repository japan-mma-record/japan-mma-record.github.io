This featured the semi-final playoffs for the [[Heavyweight]] and Women's [[Featherweight]] divisions.{{Cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/mma/story/_/id/37998206/pfl-sets-final-two-playoff-dates-matchups-held-ny|title=PFL sets final two playoff dates, matchups to be held in N.Y.|date=July 12, 2023|work=[[ESPN]]|author=Marc Raimondi}}\n\n== Background ==\nThe event was headlined by [[Renan Ferreira (fighter)|Renan Ferreira]] facing off against [[Maurice Greene (fighter)|Maurice Greene]] in an attempt to reach the heavyweight finals. Ferreira was initially scheduled to face number three seed Marcelo Nunes, but he was forced to withdraw due to an injury and was replaced by fifth-seed Greene.{{cite web|url=https://www.pflmma.com/news/2023-pfl-playoff-cards-finalized-for-new-york-city-august-18-23|title=2023 PFL PLAYOFF CARDS FINALIZED FOR NEW YORK CITY AUGUST 18 & 23|date=July 12, 2023|work=[[Professional Fighters League]]}}\n\nAt weigh-ins, Olena Kolesnyk weighed in at 147.8 pounds, 1.8 pounds over the Featherweight limit. She was fined 20 percent of his purse, which went to her opponent Pacheco, and she started the bout with a one-point subtraction. Daiqwon Buckley weighed in at 272.4 pounds, 6.4 pounds over the Heavyweight limit, leading him to being fined 20% of his purse which went to his opponent Sutherland. The bout was briefly moved to Super Heavyweight before the promotion cancelled it.

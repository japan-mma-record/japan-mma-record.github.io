About the event

CW 139 - Cage Warriors 139 took place on June 10, 2022 at San Diego, California, with 10 fights on the card. The main event featured Jordan Bailey vs Alex Trinidad in the Lightweight division.

Top fight results:

  • Jordan Bailey defeated Alex Trinidad by submission (guillotine choke) in round 1.
  • James Lynch defeated Cesar Gonzalez by TKO (doctor stoppage) in round 1.
  • Elijah Leggett defeated Brandon Laroco by unanimous decision.

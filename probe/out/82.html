Who won ACA 100: Grozny?

Wendres Carlos da Silva defeated Baysangur Vakhitov by Majority Decision in round 3 (5:00) in the Middleweight main event of ACA 100: Grozny, held Friday, October 4, 2019 at Sport Hall Colosseum, Grozny.

ACA 100: Grozny was an MMA event from Absolute Championship Akhmat, held Friday, October 4, 2019 at Sport Hall Colosseum, Grozny, Russia. A championship title was on the line. 100 is part of ACA's numbered event series. 25 fights took place on the card.

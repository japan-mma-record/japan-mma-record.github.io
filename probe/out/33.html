{{short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2022}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 284: Gracie vs. Yamauchi |image=Bellator284.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2022|08|12}} |venue=[[Sanford Pentagon]] |city=[[Sioux Falls, South Dakota]], United States |previousevent=[[Bellator 283|Bellator 283: Lima vs. Jackson]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 285|Bellator 285: Henderson vs. Queally]] }} '''''Bellator 284: Gracie vs. Yamauchi''''' was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on August 12, 2022, at [[Sanford Pentagon]] in [[Sioux Falls, South Dakota]], United States.{{Cite web |last=Newswire |first=MMA Fighting |date=2022-06-15 |title=Neiman Gracie vs. Goiti Yamauchi set to headline Bellator 284; Valentin Moldavksy, Ilima-Lei Macfarlane also on card |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/6/15/23169778/neiman-gracie-vs-goiti-yamauchi-set-to-headline-bellator-284-valentin-moldavksy-ilima-lei-macfarlane |access-date=2022-06-15 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}}{{Cite web |date=2022-06-15 |title=Bellator 284 lineup announced, including Neiman Gracie vs. Goiti Yamauchi headliner, Ilima-Lei Macfarlane, more |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/06/bellator-284-neiman-gracie-vs-goiti-yamauchi-ilima-lei-macfarlane-more |access-date=2022-06-15 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} == Background == The event marked the promotion's third visit to Sioux Falls and first since [[Bellator 265]] in August 2021. The event was headlined by a welterweight bout between [[Neiman Gracie]] and [[Goiti Yamauchi]]. A bantamweight bout between [[Josh Hill (fighter)|Josh Hill]] and Matheus Mattos was scheduled for this event.{{Cite web |last=Anderson |first=Jay |date=2022-07-12 |title=Six Fights Added to Bellator 284, Vanderford and Hill Return |url=https://cagesidepress.com/2022/07/12/six-fights-including-austin-vanderford-josh-hill-added-bellator-284/ |access-date=2022-07-13 |website=Cageside Press |language=en-US}} However, Mattos pulled out due to a knee injury requiring knee surgery and was replaced by Marcos Breno.{{Cite web |last=Cruz |first=Guilherme |date=2022-07-28 |title=Josh Hill now meets Marcos Breno at Bellator 284 with Matheus Mattos injured |url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/7/28/23282641/josh-hill-marcos-breno-matheus-mattos-withdraws-bellator-284-knee-injury |access-date=2022-07-28 |website=MMA Fighting |language=en}} A featherweight bout between [[Ilias Bulaid]] and Weber Almeida was scheduled to this event. However in July, the bout was scrapped for unknown reasons.{{Cite web |title=Bellator 284 has lost Weber Almeida vs. Ilias Bulaid. Bout scheduled for Aug. 12 in Sioux Falls, S.D. has been canceled |url=https://twitter.com/mma_kings/status/1552726634280665089 |access-date=2022-07-28 |website=Twitter |language=en}} A bantamweight bout between Sarvadzhon Khamidov and Jared Scoggins was scheduled for this event.{{Cite web |last=Anderson |first=Jay |date=2022-06-27 |title=Sarvadzhon Khamidov Set to Debut Against Jared Scoggins at Bellator 284 |url=https://cagesidepress.com/2022/06/27/sarvadzhon-khamidov-set-debut-jared-scoggins-bellator-284/ |access-date=2022-08-02 |website=Cageside Press |language=en-US}} However, the bout was scrapped for unknown reasons A middleweight bout between [[Austin Vanderford]] and Anthony Adams was scheduled for this event.{{Cite web |last=King |first=Nolan |date=2022-07-12 |title=Bellator 284 adds Austin Vanderford vs. Anthony Adams |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/07/bellator-284-austin-vanderford-vs-anthony-adams-sioux-falls |access-date=2022-07-12 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} However after Adams pulled out of the bout 2 weeks before the event, Aaron Jeffery stepped in on short notice.{{Cite web |last=Hannoun |first=Farah |date=2022-08-04 |title=With Anthony Adams out, Aaron Jeffery steps in to face Austin Vanderford at Bellator 284 |url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/08/bellator-284-austin-vanderford-vs-aaron-jeffery-with-anthony-adams-out-injury-mma-fight-booking-matchup |access-date=2022-08-04 |website=MMA Junkie |language=en-US}} At weigh ins, three fighters missed weight; [[Ilima-Lei Macfarlane]], who was three pounds over the division non-title fight limit at 129 lbs, [[Justine Kish]] who came in at 128.4 lbs for their flyweight bout, and Nick Perez who came in at 157.4 lbs for his lightweight bout. Macfarlane and Kish were fined a percentage of their purses and the bouts continued at catchweights, while Perez's bout against Isaiah Hokit was scrapped.{{Cite web |date=2022-08-11 |title=Bellator

{{short description|Bellator mixed martial arts event in 2021}} {{Infobox MMA event |name=Bellator 270: Queally vs. Pitbull 2 |image=Bellator270.jpg |image_size= |promotion=[[Bellator MMA]] |date={{Start date|2021|11|5}} |venue=[[3Arena (Dublin)|3Arena]] |city=[[Dublin]], Ireland |previousevent=[[Bellator 269|Bellator 269: Fedor vs. Johnson]] |followingevent=[[Bellator 271|Bellator 271: Cyborg vs. Kavanagh]] }} '''Bellator 270: Queally vs. Pitbull 2''' (also known as '''''Bellator Dublin''''') was a [[mixed martial arts]] event produced by [[Bellator MMA]] that took place on November 5, 2021 at [[3Arena (Dublin)|3Arena]] in [[Dublin]], Ireland. The event marked Bellator's first return to Irish soil since February 2020.{{Cite web|date=2021-07-21|title=Bellator zeroes in on James Gallagher vs. Patchy Mix grudge match in Ireland|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/07/bellator-ireland-james-gallagher-vs-patchy-mix|access-date=2021-07-21|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} == Background == A lightweight bout between #1 ranked [[Patricky Pitbull]] and #4 ranked [[Peter Queally (fighter)|Peter Queally]] served as the headliner for this event. This was a rematch of their first bout that was stopped by the doctor due to a cut on Patricky at [[Bellator 258]].{{Cite web|date=2021-08-24|title='Bellator 270: Gallagher vs. Mix' official for Nov. 5 in Dublin, adds 'Pitbull' vs. Queally rematch|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2021/08/bellator-270-james-gallagher-vs-patchy-mix-official-for-dublin-adds-patricky-pitbull-vs-peter-queally|access-date=2021-08-24|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} Originally set to be the co-headliner, the bout was promoted to the main event on October 6, 2021, when Patricky Pitbull's younger brother and [[List of Bellator MMA champions#Lightweight World Championship|Bellator MMA Lightweight World Champion]] [[Patrício Pitbull]] announced he was vacating the title, not wanting to get in the way of his older brothers title aspirations. Patricky Pitbull vs. Peter Queally was for the vacant Bellator Lightweight World Championship.{{cite web|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/10/6/22712535/patricio-pitbull-vacates-lightweight-title-patricky-pitbull-peter-queally-title-fight-bellator-270|title=Patricio Pitbull vacates lightweight title; Peter Queally vs. Patricky Pitbull elevated to title fight at Bellator 270|work=MMAFighting.com|date=October 6, 2021|author=Mike Heck|access-date=October 6, 2021}} A bantamweight bout between [[Patchy Mix]] and James Gallagher served as the co-headliner for this event.{{Cite web|last=Lee|first=Alexander K.|date=2021-08-24|title=Bellator announces return to Dublin headlined by James Gallagher vs. Patchy Mix|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2021/8/24/22639302/bellator-announces-return-to-dublin-headlined-by-james-gallagher-vs-patchy-mix|access-date=2021-08-25|website=MMA Fighting|language=en}} The first scheduled booking between the two 135-pound contenders was supposed to take place at Bellator 258 on May 7, 2021. However, Gallagher withdrew from the bout for undisclosed reasons.

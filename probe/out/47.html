Who won OKTAGON 22: Vémola vs Ďatelinka?

Karlos Vemola defeated Milan Ďatelinka by Submission in round 1 (2:12) in the Middleweight main event of OKTAGON 22: Vémola vs Ďatelinka, held Saturday, March 27, 2021 at Zoner BOBYHALL, Brno.

OKTAGON 22: Vémola vs Ďatelinka was an MMA event from Oktagon MMA, held Saturday, March 27, 2021 at Zoner BOBYHALL, Brno, Czechia. The card streamed as a Pay-Per-View. 22 is part of OKTAGON's numbered event series. 10 fights took place on the card.

marked the promotion's seventh visit to Dublin and first since [[Bellator 270]] in November 2021. A [[Bellator Middleweight World Championship]] bout between current two-time champion (also former [[List of Strikeforce champions#Light Heavyweight Championship|Strikeforce Light Heavyweight Champion]]) [[Gegard Mousasi]] and undefeated contender [[Austin Vanderford]] headlined the event.{{Cite web|title=Bellator MMA {{!}} Bellator Returns to Dublin Feb. 25 With Middleweight World Championship Fight Between Gegard Mousasi and Austin Vanderford|url=https://www.bellator.com/article/Bellator-returns-to-dublin-Mousasi-Vanderford|access-date=2021-12-16|website=www.bellator.com|language=en-US}} A middleweight bout between [[Fabian Edwards]] and Marian Dimitrov was scheduled for this event. However, the bout was scrapped a week before for unknown reason.{{Cite web|last=Andy Stevenson|date=2022-02-15|title=Fabian Edwards vs Marian Dimitrov Cancelled from Bellator 275 Dublin Card|url=https://mymmanews.com/fabian-edwards-vs-marian-dimitrov-cancelled-from-bellator-275-dublin-card/|access-date=2022-02-15|website=MyMMANews|language=en-US}} Edwards was rebooked against former [[UFC Light Heavyweight Champion]] [[Lyoto Machida]] at [[Bellator London]].{{Cite web|date=2022-02-16|title=Lyoto Machida vs. Fabian Edwards joins Bellator London lineup on May 13|url=https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/2022/02/bellator-london-lyoto-machida-vs-fabian-edwards-booked-may-13|access-date=2022-02-16|website=MMA Junkie|language=en-US}} A lightweight bout between [[Peter Queally (fighter)|Peter Queally]] and Kane Mousah and welterweight bout between Stefano Paternò and Luca Poclit were expected to take place at the event. However, Queally and Paternò both pulled out off their bouts due to injury.{{Cite web|last=Heck|first=Mike|date=2022-02-21|title=Injury scratches Peter Queally from Bellator 275; Leah McCourt vs. Sinead Kavanagh promoted to co-main event|url=https://www.mmafighting.com/2022/2/21/22944441/injury-scratches-peter-queally-from-bellator-275-leah-mccourt-sinead-kavanagh-co-main-event|access-date=2022-02-21|website=MMA Fighting|language=en}} At the weigh-ins, Jamie Hay missed weight for his bout, weighing in at 146.8 pounds, .8 pounds over the featherweight non-title fight limit.
